# Plan 04 Summary: owner review of the skin-tone contact sheets

## What was done

- The full synthetic grid (tones I-VI, full-range and dull scenes, both panel sizes, fill and bands, eight parameter sets) was rendered into a scratch directory outside the repository, together with the measures table.
- Contact sheets for tones I, III and VI were shown to the owner.

## Checkpoint outcome

The owner answered "I don't know": no judgement of how the skin tones look, and no real portraits were tried. The default parameters were therefore kept unchanged, and the next plan records plainly that real faces were not tried.

## Correction to a figure given to the owner

The owner was told that hue drift stayed under 4 degrees. That is true for portrait renders and for landscape bands only (at most 2.8 degrees). Landscape fill drifts up to 8.8 degrees on tones II to IV. The cause is not investigated; it is listed as a follow-up in the rendering document.
