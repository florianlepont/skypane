Routes (paths from `console_routes`): `LOCATIONS_ROUTE` GET, `LOCATIONS_ADD_ROUTE` POST, `LOCATION_ACTION_PREFIX` with `/edit` and `/delete` POST. Access is `routes.SIGNED_IN` for all four (both roles); ownership is a row-level check inside the handler: a user may act only on rows with `owner_user_id` equal to their user id, an admin on every row including ownerless ones; a foreign or unknown id answers the identical unknown-path 404 (never a flash that confirms existence). POSTs are `csrf=True`. Location ids in paths are decimal and validated before any lookup.

Policy numbers (owner delegated them to the planner): per-user cap 10 locations (an admin is not capped); name stripped of control characters and at most 60 characters (UI-SPEC); latitude and longitude parsed with `float()`, required finite (`math.isfinite`), range-checked and stored rounded to 4 decimals (about 11 m; third-party rounding rules belong to Phase 53 and are noted in the hand-off); time zone validated through `ZoneInfo` and chosen from a `<select>` of IANA zones with Europe/Paris default; mutation rate limit for users: a module-level `LockoutThrottle(limit=20, lockout_s=600)` keyed by the user id string, calling `record_failure` once per create, edit or delete attempt (every mutation counts), and a locked user gets a warning flash with no change. France-only bounds are NOT enforced here.
Audit actions `location.add`, `location.update` (also used with target type device for an assignment, detail naming the location id), `location.delete`; outcome denied for refusals, written with `audit.record_in` in the action's transaction or `audit.record_standalone` after a rollback.
