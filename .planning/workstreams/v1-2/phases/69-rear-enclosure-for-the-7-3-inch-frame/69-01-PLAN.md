  <name>Task 1: Write the measurement sheet (empty values table)</name>
  <files>hardware/ENCLOSURE-MEASUREMENTS.md</files>
  <action>
Create `hardware/ENCLOSURE-MEASUREMENTS.md` in English, in the style of `hardware/SECOND-HARDWARE-MEASUREMENTS.md`: a short rules section, a "Measurements and the design value each one replaces" table (columns: #, Item, How to take it, Design value (source), Replaces), then a "Values table" (columns: Item, Value, Date, Method (tool, how), Status measured or design value, not measured) and a "Notes" section. Rules section states, per D-01: only a reading the owner reports goes in; an empty row stays "design value, not measured"; every filled row needs date, tool and method actually used; Claude never fills a value.

Rows, in the order of D-04 (all tool: calliper, scale for masses; no printer needed). Design values and their source are copied from `hardware/ENCLOSURE-3D-DESIGN.md` and 69-CONTEXT.md, each labelled "design value, not measured":
1. Screen outline length x width (about 170.2 x 111.2 mm, vendor drawing). Method: measure the bare panel glass at its widest points, three readings each, record the mean and the spread.
2. Screen thickness (about 0.91 mm, vendor drawing). Method: calliper on the panel edge away from the ribbon.
3. Screen active area (160 x 96 mm, vendor drawing). Method: measure the lit/coloured area edge to edge, and the offset of the active area from each outline edge (four offsets, needed for centring).
4. Frame rear rebate length x width (about 133.4 x 183.4 mm, IKEA 3D model). Method: calliper inside the rebate, at both ends and the middle of each side.
5. Frame free depth behind the screen up to the wooden edge (22.7 mm, IKEA 3D model). Method: depth gauge or calliper rod, with the glass and the mat in place, four corners.
6. Mat thickness (1.2 mm assumed) and mat opening (about 158 x 94 mm to be cut by the owner; record the size actually cut).
7. Metal tab: depth behind the screen (about 12.6 mm, IKEA 3D model), thickness, and how many tabs per side.
8. Screen ribbon length from the panel edge to the board connector, as plugged (24 mm assumed).
9. Battery cable length from the cell to the JST-PH plug (under 105 mm per the owner, exact value unknown) and whether a JST-PH extension is needed.
10. Battery outer dimensions (8 x 50 x 60 mm, vendor value) and mass.
11. Mass of the board (EE04 board alone), the screen, and the antenna with its cable.
12. Mass of the IKEA frame as it will hang (frame, glass, mat), and of the empty frame.

Add a "Design checks to redo after the masses are in" note: closed meshes, no support, wall >= 0.8 mm, centre of gravity on the landscape hook axis all used estimated masses (tray about 57 g, lid about 48 g, stand about 11 g, shim about 5 g, design values, not measured) and must be rerun by the owner after the measurements. Leave every Value, Date and Method cell empty and every status "design value, not measured". Do not mention plan or phase IDs in comments of any file; this is a document, keep plain prose.

<task type="checkpoint:human-action" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 2: Owner measures the real parts</name>
  <files>hardware/ENCLOSURE-MEASUREMENTS.md</files>
  <action>
Ask the owner (short message, in French per D-02) to take the 12 measurements in the order of the sheet, screen first, then frame, then ribbon, cable and masses, with a calliper and a kitchen or letter scale (0.1 g if possible). Battery safety: measure the cell with its connector unplugged from the board; do not squeeze, bend or puncture it.

Report-back format (one line per row, copy as is): `row number | value with unit | date | tool and method actually used | "unable" if not taken`. Example: `1 | 170.3 x 111.1 mm (3 readings, spread 0.1) | 2026-11-20 | calliper on bare glass | `. Also ask for the mat opening actually cut and any surprise (a tab that bends, a rebate that is not square).

When the owner reports: copy each reported row verbatim into the Values table with its date and method, and set status to "measured" only for rows reported with date and method; an "unable" or missing row stays "design value, not measured". Never infer, round or compute a value the owner did not give. Add a Notes entry for every row where the measured value differs from the design value by more than 0.3 mm or 5 g, naming the part of the design it affects (shim, tray, ribbon slot, battery bay, centre of gravity).
  </action>
  <verify>
    <automated>test "$(grep -v '^#' hardware/ENCLOSURE-MEASUREMENTS.md | grep -c '| measured')" -ge 1</automated>
  </verify>
  <done>Every row the owner reported is in the Values table with date, tool and method and status measured; rows not reported still read "design value, not measured". Owner signal: "measures reported".</done>
</task>
