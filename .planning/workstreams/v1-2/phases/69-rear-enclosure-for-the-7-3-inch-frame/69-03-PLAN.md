  <action>
Create `hardware/ENCLOSURE-TEST-PRINT-LOG.md` in English, with the table style of `hardware/SECOND-HARDWARE-MEASUREMENTS.md`. Sections:

1. "Print record": fields provider, order date, receipt date, filament brand and colour, build plate, nozzle, layer height, infill, print time, cost per part and total (EUR), who reported, "reported by the owner" line. All empty.
2. "Clearances measured on the print": one row each for hinge (axle hole design 1.9 mm, knuckle diameter 6 mm), screw holes (pilot 1.7 mm, through 2.4 mm), rebate fit (0.4 mm per side, design 132.6 x 182.6 mm), battery bay (0.5 mm around, plus 1.8 mm foam thickness), ribbon slot (4 x 28 mm), antenna platform (41 x 21 mm, 7 mm from the screen), shim pocket (0.5 mm around the screen), USB-C notch (13 mm), buttons and switch notch (47.5 mm), service bay (20 mm and 30 mm wide). Columns: Item, Design value (design value, not measured), Measured on the print, Fit result (fits, tight, loose, does not fit), Adjustment, Status. All empty.
3. "Adjustments": a numbered list table, columns: #, Part, Dimension, Old value, New value, Reason (the fit result and measurement), Reprint needed (yes or no). Empty.
4. "Reprint decision": owner's go or no-go and date.

Rule at top: every cell is filled only from the owner's report with date; an adjustment is recorded only if the owner changed the CAD (D-01). Mention the glue and foam used for the battery are noted when known.

<task type="checkpoint:human-action" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 3: Owner measures the print, dry-fits it, and lists the adjustments</name>
  <files>hardware/ENCLOSURE-TEST-PRINT-LOG.md</files>
  <action>
Give the owner this procedure (French, short). Dry fit with the battery unplugged from the board at every step, and never press the cell against a sharp edge.
1. With the calliper, measure each item of the clearance table on the printed parts (hole diameters, notch widths, slot, pocket, tray outside size, hinge axle and holes).
2. Fit the shim on the screen, the tray in the RÖDALM rebate (frame face down on a soft cloth), the board, ribbon, antenna and battery in the tray, the lid with two M2 screws, the hinge and the stand. Note for each item: fits, tight, loose, does not fit, and whether the battery cable or the ribbon is pinched or bent sharply.
3. For each item that is out, decide the adjustment in the CAD, update the file and re-export; list it.

Report-back format: one line per clearance row `item | measured on print | fits/tight/loose/does not fit | adjustment (old to new) or none`, then `reprint needed yes/no`, then the date of the updated blend and export if any.

Fill "Clearances measured on the print", "Adjustments" and "Reprint decision" from the report only, with date. If a reprint is needed, record it as pending and state that Plan 04 starts after the reprint is received and the same table is updated for the reprint (add a second column set, do not overwrite the first print's values). Update the changed dimensions in `hardware/ENCLOSURE-PRINT-ORDER.md` "Design changes" table by appending rows citing this log.
  </action>
