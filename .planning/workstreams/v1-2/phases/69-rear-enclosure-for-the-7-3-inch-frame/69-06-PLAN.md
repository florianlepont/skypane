- V5 Power switch reachable: toggle OFF and ON three times through the notch; the board stops and starts.
- V6 Tab grip and shake test, before any wall: cassette in the real frame, held vertical over a soft surface, gentle shake and a pull on the frame; cassette does not slip. A fail returns to the ledger (pad height, lugs).

- V8 Hang level on the wall hook: on the real wall, phone level or spirit level; report the tilt in degrees, the notch used, orientation (landscape base, portrait optional), the wall fixing type; acceptance: level within 1 degree after notch adjustment (design value). Wall type and rating are unknown: record the type; no rating is claimed.
- V9 Hook creep: pencil line against the screw head, calliper reading and photo at hanging and again after one week; the notch bottom has not moved (calliper) and ridges are intact; warm wall or sun noted.
- V10 Stand tilt test: frame on the stand plate on a plank, raise the back edge slowly with a phone inclinometer on the plank; note the angle at which it starts to tip forward; acceptance: tips only at 5 degrees or more, does not slide at 5 degrees on the owner's actual surface, survives a gentle finger tap on the top edge; landscape and portrait; a foot pad is added if it slides.
- V11 Stand holds for 24 hours on furniture at the design tilt of 20 degrees without sliding or tipping; surface named.
- V12 Stand plate storage: the plate sits on its two magnets with the frame vertical and does not fall; hold observed.
- V13 RESET and the three buttons (KEY0, KEY1, KEY2): press each through the notch; RESET restarts the board, each key has the effect the owner saw on the bare board.
- V14 Frame mass as hung and the wall fixing used (kitchen scale, wall screw type and head size within the 9.5 mm head, 3.0 to 4.3 mm shank design values).
- V15 Wi-Fi: quotes the verdict recorded in `hardware/ENCLOSURE-WIFI-TEST.md` (Result copied from there, with its date); not done until that file says so.
- V16 Magnet near board: with the board powered, a magnet held at the storage position on the lid back and a refresh triggered; no changed image or reset (cross-reference to the battery checklist result if already reported).

Under the table add "Open follow-ups" (a failed row names the part that changes and appends a row to the adjustment ledger in `hardware/ENCLOSURE-TEST-PRINT-LOG.md`).
