<objective>
Reframe the image of the 7.3 inch EE04 to the visible window of the IKEA mat (owner scope decision D-14, 2026-11-24): the mat hides the edges of the screen's active area, so text and marks near the edges are cut off.

Purpose: success criterion 5 of Phase 69 (the assembled frame shows its image fully behind the mat). Server-side rendering only.
Output: a per-hardware visible window, the calibration record, tests, and the owner's check on the assembled frame.

Facts, design values and not measured by the assistant: active area 160 x 96 mm for 800 x 480 pixels (0.2 mm per pixel); mat window 140.93 x 90.27 mm as typed by the owner on 2026-11-23, about 705 x 451 pixels visible; the offsets of the active area from the outline are 4 mm left, 4 right, 4 top and 10 bottom (owner, tape measure, 2026-11-18). Where the mat window sits on the active area is not known: the enclosure is centred on the mat opening (D-12), so the position has to be read on the assembled frame.
</objective>
