## Trust Boundaries

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-69-20 | Denial of service (safety) | LiPo cell pinched, punctured or shorted by a screw tip or edge | mitigate | Dry fit with the plug disconnected and a dummy block of the pack's size; checklist rows BAT3 to BAT5 before any connection; routing photographed (photo stays with the owner); no screw tip in the cell zone |
| T-69-21 | Denial of service | reversed polarity of the JST extension destroys the board | mitigate | Multimeter polarity check on the extension before the first connection (checklist row BAT2, audit F-07); row dated before the power-on row |
| T-69-22 | Denial of service (safety) | swollen cell in a closed case | mitigate | 1 to 2 mm free thickness after closing, case not sealed (service bay and USB notch open), first charge attended, swelling check with dates; swollen cell means disconnect, no charge, no puncture |
| T-69-23 | Tampering | fit verdicts and the screw question | mitigate | Rows only from the owner's report with date; screw reuse recorded as four counts; "pass" without a date counts as not done |
| T-69-24 | Information disclosure | log | mitigate | No pack serial, order number, device identifier, token or password in the log; photos are not committed |
| T-69-25 | Denial of service (physical) | frame falls during the shake test | mitigate | Shake test over a soft surface; hanging with the front sheet forbidden until it passes |
</threat_model>
