  <files>hardware/ENCLOSURE-VALIDATION.md</files>
  <action>
Create `hardware/ENCLOSURE-VALIDATION.md` in English with two parts.

Part A, "Checklist" table (columns: #, Check, How the owner tests it, Result pass/fail/not done, Date, Owner's note): 
1. Screen sits centred behind the mat (look from the front; report the visible margin left and right and top and bottom in mm).
2. Board, battery and antenna fit in the tray with the lid closed.
3. Battery not pinched: cable and cell free after closing, no sharp bend, no load on the JST connector, checked with the lid open and closed.
4. USB-C reachable through the service bay with a real cable plugged and unplugged while mounted in the frame.
5. Power switch reachable.
6. Buttons (KEY0, KEY1, KEY2) reachable and each press works (one wake each).
7. Hangs level on the wall hooks: on the real wall, check with a spirit level or phone level; report the tilt in degrees; in landscape (base) and, if wanted, portrait.
8. Stand holds the frame on a piece of furniture for at least 24 hours (design value, owner may adjust) without sliding or tipping, at the design tilt of 20 degrees, in landscape and in portrait; report the surface.
9. The lid opens and closes ten times without breaking, the stand plate sits in its stop walls and sticks to its magnets with the frame vertical; screws hold (self-tapping; note any stripped thread, which triggers the heat-set insert fallback).
10. Frame mass as hung (reported by the owner) and the wall fixing used.

Part B, "Wi-Fi comparison", (write the validation document in plain prose, no plan or decision IDs): same place, same orientation, same access point for both runs; the frame is not moved between runs except to fit the enclosure. Run A: at least 20 wakes with the frame as is, without the enclosure. Run B: at least 20 wakes with the enclosure assembled. Source: the `rssi` column of the frame's own `device_health` history, exported with `GET /api/export/battery-history` as already done in `hardware/SECOND-HARDWARE-MEASUREMENTS.md` (read-only export for the device id of the frame, with `since` as a UTC ISO time such as `2026-10-23T04:25:00Z`, the only accepted parameter besides `device`; the route needs the export bearer token `Authorization: Bearer $SKYPANE_EXPORT_TOKEN` and answers 404 without a valid one; the token is never written to any file or message). Procedure steps: note the start and end time of each run, export, keep only rows inside the run, report the number of rows, min, median and max RSSI in dBm for each run (the owner pastes the rows or the figures; Claude computes the median from pasted rows when given them and says so). Pass criterion (design value, owner may adjust, record any change with date): median RSSI with the enclosure not worse than without by more than 3 dB. Add a results table: Run, wakes (n), start, end, min, median, max, location and access point (name, no password), battery state, Status. Add "Verdict" line: pass, fail or not done, with the difference of medians.

Everything starts empty with "not done". Do not state any result.
