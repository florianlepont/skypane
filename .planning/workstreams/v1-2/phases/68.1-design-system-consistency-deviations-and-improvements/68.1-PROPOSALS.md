# Phase 68.1 Proposals (DSY-03)

Improvements to the design system and animation ideas, each with a standalone mockup, the reason, the cost, and the effect on accessibility and performance. Nothing here is built (D-04): the only files are this document and the mockups `68.1-PROPOSAL-P01.html` to `P16.html` in this folder. Open a mockup in a browser; each shows Light and Dark at 360px wide.

## Method

The plan names the `frontend-design:frontend-design` skill. It could not be loaded through the Skill tool in the cloud session that produced this document (the owner has the plugin v1.1.1 installed locally). Its text was instead fetched from Anthropic's public plugin repository (main branch, so the version may differ from v1.1.1) and applied by hand: ground the design in the subject, a two-pass plan reviewed against the brief before building, motion only where it is purposeful, restraint ("remove one accessory"), and the guidance on writing. It serves the SkyPane design system (D-01 to D-04) and does not replace it. Where the skill invites aesthetic risk, the system's rules win: contrast, density, 44px targets, reduced motion, EN/FR parity, no uppercase label voice, neutral accent, system sans only, no serif or terracotta. Any proposal that bends a rule says so and is marked OWNER-DECISION.

Origin labels: **SKILL** = idea produced by applying the frontend-design skill; **OWNER** = the owner's notes of 2026-11-23/24 (`68.1-CHECK-B-pages.md`, B1-owner-notes); **CHECK** = an OWNER-QUESTION row or candidate from the consistency check (`68.1-DEVIATIONS.md`, "Carried to the proposal").

### Design plan (pass one) and review against the brief (pass two)

Subject: a wall frame that shows one aeroplane taking off or landing at Orly runway 3; the companion is the quiet remote for it. The audience is a household and the person who administers the fleet; the primary job is to glance and trust the state.

- Colour: the system's own, unchanged (canvas `#F1F3F6` / `#161A24`, graphite `#1F2430` / ivory `#F2EFE9`, text `#17191F` / `#F1F3F6`, status green `#16A34A` / `#4ADE80`, amber `#D97706` / `#FBBF24`).
- Type: system sans only; the aeroplane's own identifiers (callsign, registration) are the one place the type may speak.
- Layout: glass cards on the sky, one hero per page, everything else quiet.
- Principles: the page is about one aeroplane; spend boldness once; motion only where something changed.

Review: a first draft of this plan proposed entrance staggers on every card, hover lifts on every row, a theme cross-fade and a runway-grid background. Against the brief these are the generic defaults (and the system already ships a page opening, press spring and tab slider), so they were cut or turned into one moment (P13). Cut ideas are listed at the end. Chanel's accessory removed: the dotted meta line on Home (P15).

## Motion budget (D-03)

Soft budget: no cap on duration or property. Every animation must (1) be switched off by `prefers-reduced-motion` (each animated mockup contains the media block, and each proposal below states the reduced behaviour), (2) keep contrast, (3) be judged on the deployed site on a phone. In the product each duration must be a `--motion-*` token (test in `companion/test_companion_app_04.py`); the two animation proposals reuse existing tokens and add no keyframe count beyond what is stated.

## How to answer

Per proposal: **keep**, **drop** or **change** (say what). Unapproved proposals are recorded as v1.3 / v1.4 candidates. A kept proposal becomes its own small change with browser tests, English and French, at phone width. Proposals marked OWNER-DECISION break or bend a rule and need an explicit answer.

## P16 Tokenise spacing and radii (OWNER)

Mockup: `68.1-PROPOSAL-P16.html` (the scale with the off-scale values, one card before and after snapping, the four options)
- **Measured** (command run from the repo root: `python3 -I .planning/workstreams/v1-2/phases/68.1-design-system-consistency-deviations-and-improvements/scan_token_bypass.py`): 153 spacing findings (margin, padding, gap, inset and offsets) of which 23 equal an existing token (`--space-xs` 4px, `--space-sm` 8px, `--space-lg` 24px) and 130 equal none; 14 radius findings, none equal to a token (`--radius-chip` 9px, `--radius-control` 14px, `--radius-card` 22px, `--radius-sheet` 28px, `--radius-pill` 32px). The 14 radii are 10px x4, 8px x3, 12px x2, and one each of 6px, 16px, 18px, 999px and `8px 8px 4px 4px`; 13 of them are off-token px values and one (999px) is a full round. Across the 130, the repeated off-scale spacing values are 6px (28 uses in 5 files), 12px (20, 5 files), 14px (12, 5 files), 10px (9, 4 files); together 69 uses. The rest are one-off pairs and negative offsets (-8px x5, -18px x4, ...). Spread: `style.css` 68 findings, `style-display.css` 21, `style-airlines.css` 17, `style-home.css` 16, `style-admin.css` 14, `style-place.css` 10, the two console and list files 7. The stylesheets already use `var(--space-*)` 1113 times and `var(--radius-*)` 233 times, so the token scale is the norm and the literals are the exception (about 12% of spacing uses).
- **Reason:** the owner asked for the hard-coded values to sit on tokens so a spacing change is made in one place. Today 6, 10, 12 and 14 appear between the tokens with no name.
- **Options:**
  - **A** swap the 23 spacing literals that already equal a token. Pixels identical. Low risk.
  - **B** add tokens for the repeated off-scale values (for example `--space-2xs` 6px, `--space-s-m` 12px, plus 10 and 14, and radius tokens for 8, 10, 12) and use them. Pixels identical, the scale grows from 7 to about 11 steps.
  - **C** snap the near values to the nearest existing token (6 to 8 or 4, 10 and 12 to 8 or 16, 14 to 16; radii 8, 10, 12 to `--radius-chip` or `--radius-control`). Visible change of up to 4px per edge, so cards and rows grow or shrink. **OWNER-DECISION.**
  - **D** leave as is and record why: most literals sit in dense, fitted controls (chips, pills, menus, the 44px-target rows), and the scale already covers about 88% of uses.
- **Recommendation (for discussion only):** A now, then B only for the four repeated values (6, 10, 12, 14), and not C. A is free of visual risk and finishes the DEV-14 and DEV-16 TOKEN-EXISTS leftovers; B gives the one-place edit the owner wants without moving a pixel; C buys a smaller scale at the price of re-judging every card on a phone. Skip tokens for the one-off pairs and negative offsets (they are optical nudges and `calc()` against fixed widths).
- **Cost:** A touches 8 stylesheets and about 23 declarations; B adds about 7 tokens in the token block (four theme blocks are not involved, spacing is theme-free) and rewrites about 69 spacing and 10 radius declarations in 5 files; C is B plus a review of every page at 360 and 1280. Tests: the render baseline `companion/testdata/render_baseline.json` is regenerated with the repo tool and `--write` for A and B only to confirm it does not change; for C it changes and needs review. Browser tests at 360 in EN and FR on the pages touched. A and B: small to medium. C: large. The 80-line function gate and the comment-history guard are not affected (CSS only).
- **Accessibility:** touch targets must not shrink: the 44px minimums are `min-height` and stay as they are; C must be checked on chips and menu rows whose padding is a literal. No colour or motion change.
- **Performance:** none (CSS custom properties, a few bytes of token definitions).
- **Rule conflicts:** none for A and B. C changes a layout that earlier phases tuned on a phone; the recorded decisions for those values are not revisited here. Fleet files are in open PR #566, so edits there wait for that merge.
- **From the skill:** no.

## Ideas considered and cut (the skill's "remove one accessory")

- Fade-and-slide entrance on every card and a hover lift on every row: the skill's own tell for generated pages; the system already has a page opening and a press spring.

- A second display typeface: breaks "system sans only".
- Shimmer on rows: the system has a loading sweep; no new loading state was found.
