# Phase 68.1 Plan 21: Hover wash visible in Dark (P10) Summary

`--color-control-wash` is now theme-aware: Light stays `rgba(23, 25, 31, 0.045)` exactly, Dark and Auto-dark are `rgba(241, 243, 246, 0.08)` (8% of the text colour). Hovering a row button now shows in Dark.

## Measurement on the real surfaces (before the value was fixed)

Method: Chromium, real pages, painted pixels (1px screenshot clip at the top centre of the control, resting versus hovered), contrast by the WCAG formula; candidate values injected live as the token to read the real hover pixel. Light is the reference step.

| Consumer (page) | Surface (rest) | Light hover | Light step | Dark rest | Dark today | Dark step today | 6% | **8%** | 10% | 12% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `.row-menu__toggle` (Users list) | `#FFFFFF` / `#1C222D` | `#F5F5F5` | 1.09:1 | `#1C222D` | `#1B212C` | 1.012 | 1.169 | **1.237** | 1.336 | 1.415 |

Findings:
- The real Dark card is the opaque `--glass-solid` `#1C222D` (the popover surface is `#151922`, the table header `#262C37`); the glass fell back to solid on these pages, so the "about `#292B34`" translucent figure of the plan does not occur on the measured consumers. The mockup's solid `#1C222D` was the right surface for the card.
- Today's value gives 1.008 to 1.015:1 in Dark (invisible); Light reference is 1.09 to 1.094:1.
- 8% gives 1.222 to 1.247:1 in every consumer, at least the Light step and far from boundary-like (a 3:1 boundary is not claimed). The starting value passes the plan's own adjustment rule, so it was kept; no other value was needed.
- Text on the hovered fill: tested at 4.5:1 or more for each consumer in both themes (the faint 70%-ink label of `.airlines-act` included).
- Consumers not measured: `.filter-chip` scroll-chip hover (same surface family as the Flights filter bar, needs a seeded Flights page at the narrow breakpoint) and the `button` fallback (the `color-mix()` line right after the token overrides it, so the token is never what a supporting browser paints for a bare button). Reported as a gap, not asserted.

## What changed

- `style.css`: `--color-control-wash` declared in the four theme blocks (Light default `:root` kept, Auto-dark `prefers-color-scheme` block, explicit Light, explicit Dark); comment rewritten. No component rule changed.
- `design_system_tokens.json`: the token added to `colors.light` and `colors.dark`; drift test green.
- Skill: `tokens-and-theming.md` (token moved from the "one value" list to the themed table, measured steps recorded), `SKILL.md` "as of" line.
- Render baseline regenerated with `--write`: hash-only (every page's `style.css?v=` hash).

## Tests

`companion/test_browser_hover_wash.py` (red first: 6 Dark failures, Light passing): the token per theme context (explicit Light, explicit Dark, Auto light, Auto dark) and Light pinned; hover versus resting pixel on four real consumers in both themes (step at least 1.08, below 1.5); text on the hovered control at least 4.5:1; focus ring identical hovered or not; at 360px in EN and FR, Light and Dark, Users and the Editor: no sideways scroll, a hover changes no box (44px kept), no running animation under reduced motion.

## Deviations from Plan

- The step threshold is 1.08 rather than a literal 1.09: Light measures 1.09 after 8-bit pixel rounding, so 1.08 keeps the "at least the Light step" intent without a flaky tie.
- No GitHub draft PR opened: the work rides on the existing PR #596.
