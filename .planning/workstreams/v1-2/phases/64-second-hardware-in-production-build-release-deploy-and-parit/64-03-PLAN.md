<objective>
Give `firmware/provision.sh` the same typed live-frame guard as `flash-second.sh` (D-05, criterion 8): read the chip MAC, refuse the live frame's MAC or port, require the MAC typed back.

Open question resolved: "refuse the live frame's MAC or type". Both boards are ESP32-S3 and the silicon cannot tell its type, so "type" is realised as the declared live-frame identity: the owner-declared `SKYPANE_LIVE_FRAME_MAC` and `SKYPANE_LIVE_FRAME_PORT`, exactly as `flash-second.sh` does, plus the mandatory typed MAC. A server-side lookup of live-type rows (needs network) is not used. The limit is stated in the script header: when the variables are unset only the typed confirmation protects, and unsetting them is the deliberate override.
</objective>
