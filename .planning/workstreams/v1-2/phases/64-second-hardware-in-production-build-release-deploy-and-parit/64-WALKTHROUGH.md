## Gaps found

- Gap 1 (display bug, 2026-11-15, owner, French, administrator): on the Fleet page the action menu of the last row of the "Autres cadres" list is cut off at the bottom (the menu opens downward and is clipped by the page area), so "Re-activate" of a retired frame cannot be chosen in the console. Reproduced by the owner on a retired second-type frame; probably the same for any last row. To fix, or to carry to SEED-019 if the owner prefers. Workaround: `fleet_cli devices reactivate --id <pending id>`. Fixed in PR #434 (merged 2026-11-15); the owner re-checked on desktop on 2026-11-16: "J'ai testé sur mon ordi et c'est bon".
- Gap 2 (2026-11-15, owner, English, administrator): the frame preview on the frame's page shows a landscape picture inside a portrait-shaped bezel (screenshot).
- Gap 4 (2026-11-15, found by rendering the default giant layout hold screens of the EE04 with the repo's code, after the owner pointed out that the first images showed the old classic design): in landscape the battery-low glyph and the source-fault badge were drawn in the bottom-left corner of the giant status screens (`server/plane/render/giant/status.py`, `_paint_marks`), on top of the last line of the body text. Portrait was not affected. Fixed by PR #440 (marks at the top right) and PR #441 (shorter caption "Check the companion page"). Owner, 2026-11-16: validated on the rendered images shown together, not on a real frame (the marks only appear on a low battery or an ADS-B source fault).
- Gap 3 (2026-11-15, owner, English, administrator): on the theme page the landscape looks appear in portrait-shaped tiles (screenshot).
  Both come from the preview containers keeping a portrait shape while the picture follows the frame's landscape shape; the picture itself is right. To fix in the companion CSS and markup, with a browser test of the container shape. Fixed in PR #439; owner re-check 2026-11-16: ok.
- Remark 4 (2026-11-15, owner, English, administrator, screenshot of the Flight history page): when the page is narrowed, the search field drops under the title at some width, which looks odd. Not specific to the second type; recorded as an input of `SEED-019-console-visual-improvements.md`.

Not specific to the second type. The preview images are served with a 5-minute cache (`send_bytes(..., cache_seconds=300)` in `companion/preview_images.py`), so the reload may come from full page navigations or from preview URLs that change at each render; not investigated. Recorded as an input of `SEED-019-console-visual-improvements.md`.
