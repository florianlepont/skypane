## Gaps found

- Gap 1 (display bug, 2026-11-15, owner, French, administrator): on the Fleet page the action menu of the last row of the "Autres cadres" list is cut off at the bottom (the menu opens downward and is clipped by the page area), so "Re-activate" of a retired frame cannot be chosen in the console. Reproduced by the owner on a retired second-type frame; probably the same for any last row. To fix, or to carry to SEED-019 if the owner prefers. Workaround: `fleet_cli devices reactivate --id <pending id>`. Fixed in PR #434 (merged 2026-11-15); the owner re-checks on desktop after the deploy.
- 2026-11-15, owner: a retired frame is listed on the Fleet page while the owner is on the view of another frame (here the 13.3 inch frame's view), so it is unclear which frame the page is about. Not a failure of the second type; recorded as an input of `.planning/seeds/SEED-019-console-visual-improvements.md`.
