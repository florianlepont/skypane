# Phase 61 Plan 48: Short texts and a one-level Personalisation Summary

Owner feedback W24, W26, W30, W47, W48 and decision D35 (W27 is superseded by D35): Personalisation is one navigation (Departures, Arrivals, Special looks, Calendar), the look views are two columns on desktop, the Home notification, Special looks texts and the New special look sheet are short and the sheet preview is big, and each Account frame row carries a graphic frame.

- **One navigation (W47, D35).** `personalisation_cards` now has four views: Departures `/display` (unchanged), Arrivals `/display/arrivals` (new), Special looks, Calendar. One pill bar (plane, plane nose down, star, calendar icons; the sliding thumb and phone scroll-snap of the old bar are unchanged). The Departures/Arrivals tabs inside the Look view are gone; both views render the same look picker (`look_card_body_html(..., usage)`): the chosen type's picture and native radio table are shown, the other type's stay in the page, hidden, so the form posts both fields, the dirty-state and save behaviour are unchanged and "Same as departures" still resolves on Arrivals. `theme-preview.js` opens on `data-look-initial`. A rejected `theme_arriving` is redrawn on Arrivals (`config_page._FIELD_VIEWS`). New `layout.DISPLAY_ARRIVALS_ROUTE`; same `OWNER_DEVICE` access, device-scoped like the other views.
- **Screen view removed (D35).** Nav entry, card, "Current: ..." lines and strings dropped; `/display/screen` is still served and answers 303 to `/display` (prefix kept), through `personalisation_cards.RETIRED_VIEW_ROUTES` and one redirect route in `routes.py` (nothing added to `app.py`, which went from 1474 to 1473 lines).
- **Two columns (W48).** From 960px `.look-view` is a grid: picture left (width limited by the column and `100vh - 21rem`), picker card right. At 1280x800 both end inside the first screen (browser test). Phone keeps the stacked layout.
- **Short texts (W24, W26).** Home notice: titles and one-sentence bodies as in `61-UI-SPEC.md` (the "help lead" line merged into the body; the figure lines and the mailto link stay; the cannot-tell line removed). Special looks: no "Current" line (Calendar neither), intro "A different look for one airline, flight or plane.", empty state is one line ("No special looks yet.") above the primary button (`empty_state(compact)` omits an empty body), precedence line cut to its first sentence. New special look sheet: the preview heads the form at 44% of a phone sheet's content width with a one-line caption beside it, the foot (Cancel, Add special look, 44px) is sticky in the scrolling sheet.
- **Account frames (W30).** Each row leads with the CSS-drawn frame (`ui_shell.frame_pic_html`, the helper the frame tiles now use too), then the name, hardware or owner line, status dot with the last-seen word (`ui_shell.seen_text`), and the action. `entity_row_html` gained `extra_class`. "Add a frame" untouched.
- **Strings.** Removed (EN code, FR catalogue): `display.current_value`, `display.special_looks_one/many`, `display.calendar_connected`, `display.special_looks_empty_hint`, `look.departures_or_arrivals`, `home.notice_help_lead/plain`, `home.notice_line_cannot_tell`; shortened: the Home tips and titles, `look.override_the_look...`, `look.most_specific_wins`, `look.matching_flights_will_look_like_this`, `display.special_looks_none`.
- **Tests (adapted in place, none weakened).** `test_personalisation_page.py` (four views, ids, redirect test replaces the Screen switch test, Arrivals view and rejected-arrivals redraw added), `test_browser_personalisation.py` (new nav, desktop two-column fit at 1280x800, Arrivals view with Reset, retired address), `test_browser_display_looks.py` (views instead of tabs, saves per view, side-by-side desktop, new big-preview sheet test), `test_home_notice.py`, `test_browser_home_notice.py`, `test_browser_overhaul_sweep.py` (notice brevity, Arrivals in the sweep, account dots), `test_browser_account_frames.py`, `test_account_page.py`, route and access pins (`test_access_matrix.py`, `test_device_routes.py`, `test_route_table.py` 25 to 26 owner routes, `test_user_view_http.py`), `test_config_page_04/04b/05.py`, `test_display_looks.py`, `test_display_detection_card.py`, `test_personalisation_special_looks.py`, `test_browser_ux_03/04.py`, `test_companion_app_05.py`. `render_baseline.json` regenerated (Arrivals added to the snapshot request lists in `test-support/companion_render_snapshot.py`); the sign-in digests did not move.
