<objective>
Turn the browser job and the non-browser job into balanced shard matrices so the slowest job fits the 10-minute target.

Purpose: the summed test time is about 98 minutes (70 browser, 28 other); on 4 workers per job, 4 browser shards plus 2 non-browser shards put about 4.4 and 5.6 minutes of test time in each job. Setup (about 1.5 minutes) plus aggregate (about 2 minutes) then keeps the critical path near 9 minutes. The numbers are re-measured; counts are adjusted by evidence (CIS-01, CIS-02).
Output: weights file, matrix in ci.yml, per-shard artifact names, timing record.
</objective>
