## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** quick run command on the touched files.
- **After every plan wave:** full suite command, the static gates and the must-not-break set; the baseline is regenerated by the one plan designated for the wave and its diff reviewed (only the expected pages change).
- **Before `/gsd:verify-work`:** full suite green with `SKYPANE_REQUIRE_BROWSER=1`; EN/FR x light/dark x 360/1280 sweeps (`test_browser_overhaul_sweep`, `test_browser_french_fit`, `test_browser_contrast_edges`); owner validation on the deployed companion in English and French (plan 32).
- **Max feedback latency:** 68 seconds for the quick run.
