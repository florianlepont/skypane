Health page: the Battery card

Mockup for the owner. Current card against the proposal, on the real shell, phone 390 px and desktop 1440 px, light and dark, English and French. Not approved yet.

What changed against the current Battery card (every datum kept; nothing is added except a Level column in the readings table, derived from the voltage the card already has).
  • One level, stated once. The big gauge carries the percentage; the voltage sits beside it as the secondary value. The header repeat (“77% · 3969 mV”) and the ring line (“≈ 77% · 3969 mV — 20 Nov 11:46 (25s ago)”) are gone; the time and the age become one quiet “Last reading” line.
  • The ring becomes a real gauge: 270° arc, 112 px (96 px on phone), the level in the centre, a tick at the low-battery mark (20 %). Green above, orange below the threshold.
  • Verdict = a status pill with an icon and the words (check mark + “Level looks normal”; warning triangle + “Level is low”). Colour only reinforces. Charging is a pill with a bolt in the card header, shown only when present.
  • Percentage / Voltage = one segmented control (same look as the design system’s segmented control: one track, the active segment filled, two 44 px segments, radiogroup semantics). It sits on the chart title row, so it reads as the chart’s switch, not the card’s.
  • Lighter chart. No grey box. Light horizontal grid, area under the curve fading from the accent, y-range fitted to the data (a voltage curve between 3900 and 4200 mV now has a visible slope), 3 date ticks, an end marker with its latest value. Axis and label text 13 to 14 px (was 10 to 11), y labels sit on the grid lines so the plot uses the full card width.
  • Threshold: a dashed orange line (not colour alone: dashed + a 13 px label on the line), the zone below it tinted. When the data stays far above it, the axis is not stretched down to 3000 mV; a caption under the chart says the threshold is below the range shown. The axis includes the threshold as soon as the data comes within 1.5 steps of it.
  • Readings table: the disclosure becomes a 52 px full-width row with a chevron and the count; table gets Time, Voltage, Level, the newest row emphasised.
