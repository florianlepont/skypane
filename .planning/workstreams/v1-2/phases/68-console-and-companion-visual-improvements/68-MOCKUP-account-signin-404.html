Account, sign-in and 404

Phase 68, remarks R19 to R24. Each block shows today (as served now) against the proposal. Sample names and addresses are invented. Use the switches for phone (360 px) or desktop (1280 px), English or French, light or dark.

1. Account frame rows (R19, R20, R21)

Proposed: the action (Manage access, or Leave frame on a shared frame) moves into the row as a 44 px control at the right, so the row loses the extra link line and its odd left margin. On phone it is an icon with an accessible name; on desktop it carries its label. The administrator sees no hardware type. Battery is told by the dot only.

2. Credits for signed-in people (R23)

Proposed: an "About" group on the Account page with one Credits row. It reaches every signed-in person (users: Account tab; administrators: Account row of the More sheet), needs no navigation, route or access change, and the Credits page already offers "Back to Account".

3. Sign-in card, "First visit" (R22)

Proposed: the hint is not a block of its own above the button any more. It is one calm centered line under the button, with a small mail icon, same words. The button keeps first place on the card. The e-mail word no longer breaks at its hyphen in French. As today, the hint is hidden when an error notice is shown.

4. 404 page (R24)

Proposed: the page sits on the existing glass card with an inline SVG illustration (no new asset or font, colours from the tokens, so it follows light and dark), a friendly line and the existing button at 44 px. Two options to choose from. The heading "Page not found." and the button label (Back to Home, Back to Account or Back to sign in, by context) are unchanged.
