<objective>
Ship the three runway diagram images the developer produced, and simplify the Config page's runway picker to name each runway the way Orly's own signage does.

Purpose: Two loose ends from Phase 06.4 close here. First, `companion/static/RUNWAY-IMAGES.md` shipped a drop-in asset contract with no assets — the picker has been rendering its text-only D-03 fallback ever since. The three PNGs now exist in the working tree, unversioned, so no deploy would ever carry them; committing them is literally the whole remaining step. Second, the picker currently labels runways by heading-pair ID ("07/25", "06/24", "02/20"), which is aviation-correct but is not what the airport itself calls them. The official Aéroport de Paris runway-works diagram labels them Piste 3, Piste 4 and Piste 2, and that is the vocabulary a person standing in front of the frame — or reading Orly's own notices — will recognise.

Output: three tracked PNG assets, and a three-line label change plus a dated provenance comment in `server/device_config.py`. No test file changes. No render/firmware/deploy changes.
</objective>
