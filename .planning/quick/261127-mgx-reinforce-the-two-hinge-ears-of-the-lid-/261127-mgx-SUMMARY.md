# Quick 261127-mgx: reinforce the two hinge lugs of the lid

Lugs are 6 mm thick (was 5, grown outward only), stand on a 1.5 mm socle, with a rounded socle, and 3 mm concave side fillets; hole, crush ribs, throat and leg unchanged. The socles are trimmed to stay 1 to 2 mm from the hook blocks and the lid edge.

## Verified on the model (Blender 5.3.0, local MCP)

- The unchanged script reproduced the committed lid, leg and tray exactly before the edit (8010, 524, 6644 triangles).
- Lid 8712 triangles, 40306.8 mm3 (+325.6), closed, no open edge, in the blend, the STL and the 3mf mesh; leg and tray unchanged.
- Leg swept 0 to 48 degrees: no contact with the new geometry outside the pin hole zone.

## Not measured

All strength figures are design values. Nothing printed. A possible cause of the break (0.3 mm snap interference on thin arms) is recorded in the print order and left unchanged by request.
