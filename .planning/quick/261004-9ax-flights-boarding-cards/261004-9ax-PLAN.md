# 261004-9ax: Flights phone tiles as boarding-pass cards (Direction B, compact)

## Objective

Below 960 px each Flights row becomes a compact boarding-pass card
(owner-approved Direction B, mockup in the session scratchpad
`sketches-flights/flights-B.html`). The desktop table at >= 960 px is unchanged.
The owner wants it as compact as legibility allows, with more flights per
390x844 screen than the mockup.

## Card anatomy (server-rendered, no inline styles)

- **Header row:** callsign in the mono identifier style, airline muted under it.
  An unresolved airline shows "Unknown airline" in italics plus the existing
  "Name this airline" link. A rowless hex keeps the "no callsign" note. The
  picture action sits top-right: an icon-only real `<a href="/gallery/...">`
  with `data-view-panel-*`, a 44 px target, and the accessible name
  "View picture of X" / "Voir l'image de X". It is omitted when there is no
  picture.
- **Route row (one row):** origin, a dashed track with a plane glyph, and
  destination. The direction label sits under the plane, inside the same row,
  so it adds no extra row. The home end (origin when departing, destination
  when arriving) is muted. A missing route falls back to "Route unavailable".
- **Tear line:** notches cut into the card's own border, decorative only.
- **Stub row:** a wide artwork plate on the left, or a dashed "No illustration"
  placeholder (new EN/FR string). The time and relative age are right-aligned.
- **Slim day headers** ("Today · 3 Oct"), built from the existing
  `paris_day`/`day_label`, with no new query.
