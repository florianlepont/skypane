# Phase quick/260905-e04 Plan 01: Add two band themes with a tinted dither Summary

Added `band_blue_field` and `band_red_field` to `device_config.THEMES` — the first diagonal-band
themes whose background field is a dithered tint of the band's own hue instead of White. Extended
the test suite to prove the dominance guard explicitly for both themes, fixed prose/counts the
change falsified, and produced the four preview renders the developer needs to review before an
on-glass session. **This plan's blocking checkpoint (Task 3) has NOT been closed** — the automated
half (full suite + previews) is done, but the on-glass verdict is the developer's step and has not
yet been returned.

## What was built

**Task 1 — Registry entries + registry-harness extension (commit dc45f3b)**

- `server/device_config.py`: added `band_blue_field` (`departing_index`/`arriving_index`=IDX_BLUE,
  `ink_index`=IDX_WHITE, `dithered`=True, `weight`="bold", `band_index`=IDX_BLUE,
  `band_dithered`=False) and `band_red_field` (same shape, IDX_RED). Base-canvas fields copied from

  `_theme_band_index_matches_registry_or_none`, `_theme_band_dithered_matches_registry_or_false`)
  with the two new ids, zero change to the 16 pre-existing ids' expected values. Added ONE new

## CHECKPOINT STILL OPEN — developer verdict required

**Task 3's blocking on-glass checkpoint (DP-7) has not been closed.** The automated half (full
suite green, four preview PNGs produced) is the precondition for asking the developer, not the
answer. Per the plan's `<on_glass_handoff>`, this is explicitly not something an agent can close —
Phase 9's 09-04 session is direct evidence that a screen preview mis-called band/text contrast on
real Spectra 6 glass for every band colour tested (Blue, Green, and Red), and tone-on-tone at 40%
lightening is exactly the class of judgment a monitor has already been shown to get wrong here.

**What the developer needs to do (from the plan's `<how-to-verify>`):**

1. Look at the four PNGs above as a cheap pre-filter.
2. Set the device's theme to `band_blue_field` via the companion Settings picker, let the panel
   take a real refresh, then on the physical Spectra 6 glass judge:
   - Does the solid blue band read as a distinct band against the light-blue field, or does
     tone-on-tone at 40% lightening collapse the edge?
   - Is the white in-band text legible on the solid band?
   - Is the white ink OUTSIDE the band (top labels, previous-flight card, battery/source-fault
     indicators) legible on the dithered light-blue field? (This is the DP-2 decision under test —
     `blue_light` passed the same question in 08-06, but never had a band crossing it.)
   - Does any in-band text overflow onto the field? (Would be readable rather than invisible on a
     tinted field, per F-4, but still wrong if it happens.)
3. Repeat step 2 for `band_red_field` — with particular attention, since 09-04 is direct evidence
   that a screen preview mis-called contrast for Blue, Green, AND Red alike.
4. Return the verdict: "approved" if both themes pass on glass, or describe exactly what failed and
   on which theme. A clean full-suite run or good-looking preview PNGs are explicitly NOT
   sufficient to treat this as approved.

If a theme fails on glass, the fix surface is small and lives entirely in its registry row
(`band_dithered`, `dithered`, `ink_index`, or `weight` in `server/device_config.py`).
