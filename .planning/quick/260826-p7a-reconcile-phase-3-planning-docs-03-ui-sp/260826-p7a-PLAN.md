- `_assert_legal_palette(canvas, bg_idx)` replaced 03-02's spatially-scoped `_assert_palette_contract()`. It asserts only two things: every index on the panel is one of the six legal indices, and `bg_idx` is the single most common index. There is no longer any spatial Yellow/Red reservation.
