# Quick 261003-tq4: Home status polish

Owner feedback on Home, three changes.

1. **Next-update line + cadence.** On time the line reads "Next update in 4 min"
   (FR "Prochaine mise à jour dans 4 min"), no clock, no "≈". Overdue and held
   wordings stay. The cadence `<details>` is removed; its text moves into an
   info button next to the line with a CSS-only tooltip (hover, focus), an
   `aria-describedby` + `role="tooltip"` pair, a 44 px hit area and a new
   `icon-info` sprite glyph. Old cadence strings are deleted.
2. **Image caption.** Remove the caption under the latest image; the alt text
   carries the flight identity instead. Delete orphaned strings and CSS.
3. **Animated dot.** CSS-only halo pulse on the status dot: green when the
   screen is on, amber for overdue / battery hold, none for quiet hours and
   screen off. Duration is a new documented token exception
   (`--motion-dot-pulse`) spendable only by the dot halo rule; reduced motion
   turns the animation off.
