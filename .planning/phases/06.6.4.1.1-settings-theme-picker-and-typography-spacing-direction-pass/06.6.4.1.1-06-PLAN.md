  <what-built>
  The whole 06.6.4.1.1 direction pass, across five plans:
  - **Settings › Theme (UIR-01/02):** 16 stacked 44px native radios in a bare square
    fieldset are replaced by a wrapping grid of ~160px chips. Each chip carries a real
    `server.plane.render` panel preview of that theme (rendered server-side against one
    fixed fictional scene, cropped to a thin band, cached on disk), the theme's two palette
    swatches, its name, and an accent border plus a check glyph when selected. The group
    now sits in the same `.theme-status` card Runway and Diagnostic LED use.
  - **Type (UIR-20/21/22/23):** the serif stack now leads with Iowan Old Style/Charter
    instead of Georgia; the page title is 32px semibold with tighter tracking; section
    headings are 22px regular; nested card titles inside a section are 16px sans semibold;
    the wordmark is 24px serif semibold; and every label in the app — stat-tile captions,
    table headers, mobile card labels, filter counts, pills, chips — is now one voice:
    12px sans semibold uppercase.
  - **Spacing and density (UIR-24/25/26):** one 24px sibling-card gap everywhere; 24px card
    padding at ≥960px and 16px below; a measure cap on one-line card text; section intros
    stack on phones; History's secondary text is 13px at 75%; buttons are 36px/14px below
    960px and unchanged at 30px/13px above it; the "Saved —" banner now renders below the
    page title instead of above it.
  </what-built>
