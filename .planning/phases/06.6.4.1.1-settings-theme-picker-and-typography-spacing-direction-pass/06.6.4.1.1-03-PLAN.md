<objective>
Land the spacing/density half of the direction pass in `companion/static/style.css`:
one sibling-card gap (D-14), roomier desktop card padding (D-15), a measure cap on
one-line card text (D-16), stacked section intros on phones (D-19), and History's
small-text density tweak plus a mobile-only button size (D-18).

Purpose: UIR-25's finding is that three different sibling-card gaps coexist and that
16px padding under a large serif title on a 64px-gutter page makes cards read as tight
boxes inside a roomy page. UIR-26's is that 12px at 70% is at the edge of comfortable on
dark, and that 13px buttons feel small on a phone. CONTEXT locks the audit's fixes.

Output: spacing and density changes in `style.css`, plus new guards in
`companion/test_status_pages.py` for the two contracts that are easy to silently undo.
</objective>
