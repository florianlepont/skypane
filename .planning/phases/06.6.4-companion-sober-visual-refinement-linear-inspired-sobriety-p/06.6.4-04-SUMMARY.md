# Phase 06.6.4 Plan 04: Single-signal nav pills and a segmented theme picker Summary

**Both nav renderers moved from 44px/left-rule-plus-tint doubled active signals to 32px tinted-accent pills, and the theme picker became one bordered segmented control instead of three independent boxes — six rewritten rules, three new `:not()`-scoped hover rules, zero Python changes.**

## Performance

- **Files modified:** 1 (`companion/static/style.css`)

## Accomplishments
- Sidebar and mobile-dropdown nav links dropped their 44px floor and left-border cue, landing on a shared 32px/Label-size/pill-radius shape with the tinted-accent-pill-plus-semibold-weight as the sole active signal (D-05), plus a `:not()`-scoped inactive-hover wash that structurally cannot outrank the active tint (D-05's dark-mode-analogue gap closed for free by the two-theme `color-mix` idiom).
- D-05a resolved to option (b) — reusing the existing `--weight-semibold` token on the active link rather than adding a new 500-weight token — with the exact one-line revert to option (a) documented for the developer's closing-checkpoint confirmation.
- The theme picker's `<form class="theme-form">` became the segmented control's bordered container (2px padding, 1px border, `var(--radius-control)`); each `.theme-option` became a borderless, flex-1 28px segment with `box-shadow: none` explicitly neutralizing plan 02's submit-button inset highlight; the active segment now reuses the nav link's tinted-pill language byte-for-byte.
