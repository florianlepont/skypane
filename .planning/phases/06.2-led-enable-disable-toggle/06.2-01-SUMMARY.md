## Decisions Made

- `normalise_led_enabled()` deliberately has no registry (unlike `normalise_theme_id()`/`normalise_runway_id()`) — a boolean has no attributes beyond itself, so a `LED_STATES` registry would be pure indirection (per 06.2-RESEARCH.md's own "Alternatives Considered")
- The LED control is its own dedicated form/route (D-01) rather than a third fieldset in the existing `/config` form, resolving the "absent checkbox = unchanged vs. explicitly off" ambiguity the same way `poll_trigger_section()`'s own `/poll-now` form already does
- `led_section()` is appended as a sibling `page-section` after Poll, not inserted into the `/config` form's fieldset grid, to avoid breaking 06.3-UI-SPEC.md's locked 2-column grid rule (a comment in `render()` records this reasoning so a future executor does not "fix" it)
- `read_led_enabled()` in `stub-server/byos_server.py` is a small independent reimplementation of the read-path normalisation logic rather than an import of `server.device_config` — preserves the vendored file's stdlib-only claim and D-03's frozen-file discipline
