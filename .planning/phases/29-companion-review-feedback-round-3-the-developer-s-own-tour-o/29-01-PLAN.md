<objective>
Delete the page-wide picture-editing mode from Compagnies and make the illustration dialog own its own actions. The "Modifier les images" / "Change pictures" toggle, its editing badge, its explanatory caption, the per-card Replace button, and the `?edit=1` query plumbing are removed outright; the dialog's Replace form (and its Delete form) render on every page load and are shown or hidden per open by the visibility logic `panel-lookup.js` already contains.

Purpose: the developer's own words — *"ce bouton est pas au bon endroit et pas bien placé. typiquement quand on est dans la liste d'avion on ne le voit plus"* and *"Le bouton « remplacer l'image » pourrait être ici"* (in the lightbox). The affordance moves to where the picture is, and the door that hid it closes permanently (CFG-81).

Output: a Compagnies page with no editing mode, a dialog that always carries its actions, and both view/status harnesses rewritten so the removed distinction is no longer tested and the new unconditional behaviour is.
</objective>
