<objective>
Apply the editorial floor to the two settings pages. Ten over-length caption constants on `/display` and `/device` come down to one sentence of at most twelve words; the apply-timing clause is deleted from every card caption because the Frame strip on the same page already carries it; and anything cut that was genuinely useful reference material moves into an existing "How it works" disclosure rather than being lost. Display's Aspect section is explicitly OUT of scope — Phase 30 rebuilds that card from scratch and owns its copy — and the floor check this plan adds carries a pinned exemption list naming exactly those four captions.

Purpose: the developer's own words — *"il y a trop de texte descriptif qui servent à rien sur l'ensemble du site"* — with the runway caption quoted as his example. The 2026-09-17 audit's P1 says the same from measurement: Affichage and Appareil "répètent souvent trois idées : ce que fait le réglage, quand il s'applique et son impact", and Appareil's two simple settings occupy more than two screens before the diagnostics.

Output: two settings pages where every card says one thing, a Frame strip that is the single home of the apply-timing sentence, and a fast module-local floor check that names the page it failed on. Plan 29-06 generalises the same floor to all six routes.
</objective>
