## Three existing checks Task 2 knowingly breaks (retarget them, do not weaken them)

Adding icons puts four `<svg>` elements into Health's rendered **body**. Three checks in `companion/test_status_pages.py` currently use "is there an `<svg`" as a proxy for "is there a sparkline", which stops being true the moment any other SVG exists on the page. All three are in `companion/test_status_pages.py`; Task 2 fixes all three:

| Check | Current assertion | Why it breaks | Required fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_battery_empty_state_no_svg` | `"<svg" not in rendered` | 4 icons now render on the empty path | Assert no **sparkline**: zero `<polyline` and zero `health_page.SPARKLINE_DOT_CLASS`. Rename the function and its registered check name to say sparkline, not svg. |
| `_battery_trend_shows_all_readings_and_one_sparkline` | `rendered.count("<svg") == 1` | 1 sparkline + 4 icons = 5 | Assert `rendered.count("<svg") - rendered.count("<use") == 1` — exactly one non-icon SVG. Keep the existing `count("<polyline") == 1` assertion byte-identical; it already carries the check's real meaning. |
| `_empty_battery_history_stays_script_free` | `"<svg" not in rendered` | same as the first | Same fix as the first: assert zero `<polyline` and zero `SPARKLINE_DOT_CLASS`. Keep its `<script` and readout-id assertions exactly as they are — those are unaffected and are the check's stated subject. |

None may be deleted or have its remaining assertions loosened. Each is being made *more* precise, not less: "one sparkline" is what they always meant, and "one `<svg`" was only ever a shorthand that happened to hold.

Note that `page_shell()`'s own sprite is **not** involved — these checks call `health_page.render()`, which returns the body only. The sprite lives in the shell.
