  <name>Task 1: Pure estimation functions with known-answer tests</name>
  <files>hardware/logtools.py, hardware/test_logtools_trend.py</files>
  <behavior>
    - Constant-current synthetic export: build polls whose mV follow the curve inverse of a charge falling at exactly I mA (use device_policy knots to invert), interval 900 s, 5 days; the function returns I within 2 percent for 3000 and 10000 mAh.
    - First 24 h are ignored: a synthetic large voltage drop injected only in the first 24 h does not change the estimate.
    - Medians: a single 200 mV glitch poll inside an end window does not move the end-point median.
    - Quiet hours: polls with Paris local time in [23:30, 06:00) are excluded from the end-point medians (a synthetic night sag of 80 mV in those polls does not change the estimate); with quiet hours disabled they do; the printed quiet share counts them.
    - End-point time is the median timestamp of the selected polls; elapsed is between the two end-point times.
    - Refusals raise RunReportError: span after the 24 h cut under 48 h; fewer than 5 non-quiet polls in either end window; non-positive fall (message states the fall is below what the voltage can resolve and that no current is claimed).
    - Hypotheses: favourable = mah_per_cycle * 3600 / interval_s, central = standing_ma + wake_mah * 3600 / interval_s, both computed from the two committed JSON files; at 900 s they are about 1.95 and 8.66 (the BATTERY-MODEL.md text rounds these to "about 1.9" and "about 8.6"). 180-day allowance = capacity_mah / 4320.
    - Band classification of the estimate: at or below the allowance, between allowance and favourable hypothesis, between favourable and central, above central; each with a plain sentence. Sensitivity: mA change per 10 mV of end-point error at the start and end voltage, computed from battery_fraction.
  </behavior>
