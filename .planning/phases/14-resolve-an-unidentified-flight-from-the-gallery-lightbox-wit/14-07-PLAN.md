<objective>
Close Phase 13's UAT gap G-01: a rejected airline name the operator genuinely typed (a
path-shaped string, or one whose slug collapses to nothing usable) is currently reported back
as if the field had been left empty. Give it its own flash key, wired at the one call site
that already distinguishes every other rejection reason.

Purpose: 14-02 declared `FLASH_MANUAL_NAME_UNUSABLE` in `airlines_page.py` and explicitly
deferred wiring it to this plan ("wired into companion/app.py by plan 14-07, not by this
plan") — this plan discharges that promise. 13-CONTEXT.md's governing constraint is that the
operator is never silently misled; collapsing "you typed nothing" and "you typed something
this app can't use" onto one message is exactly that. `14-CONTEXT.md`'s Deferred Ideas
explicitly elevated G-01 into this phase's scope rather than deferring it again.

This is the phase's one necessary touch of `companion/app.py`. It is deliberately narrow:
`manual_resolutions.add_entry()`'s own validation ladder, return-code contract, and
`_handle_manual_resolve_post()`'s control flow, redirect construction and security posture
(the D-11 live-registry re-validation, the `SameSite=Strict` CSRF posture) are all unchanged —
only the flash-key *selection* for one already-existing branch is narrowed. This is why the
fix does not conflict with 14-CONTEXT.md's Phase Boundary note that the two POST routes are
"not touched" in the structural sense that note protects (no new validation, no new route, no
change to what gets persisted or rejected).

Output: one new rebound flash key with its message/role entries in `companion/app.py`, one
narrowed branch inside `_handle_manual_resolve_post()`, and two existing checks in
`companion/test_companion_app.py` extended in place to prove the two rejection reasons now
produce two distinct, correct flash keys.
</objective>
