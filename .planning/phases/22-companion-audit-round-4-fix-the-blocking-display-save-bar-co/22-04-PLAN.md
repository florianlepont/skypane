<objective>
Wire the frame's one state truth into the two places that contradict each other
today. The strip flips to "Expected since HH:MM" the instant the computed time
passes, with no grace and no quiet-hours awareness, while the Health tile uses a
three-times-interval warn threshold — a contradiction window every cycle, and a
guaranteed false alarm every single night. At the same time, rebuild the strip's
geometry: three cells measuring 119 / 165 / 69 px with a 22 px headline floating
in empty space become three cells of one height, one internal grid and one
chrome, with the update line demoted to the Emphasis role and the two switch
buttons no longer wearing the accent fill.

Purpose: CFG-26's "the frame's state reads the same everywhere" is only true if
every renderer reads the same result. This plan converts the two renderers that
sit on an accent surface on the two pages that matter most; Home and the settings
captions follow in plans 22-07 and 22-05.
Output: a strip that is honest and square, a Health tile that agrees with it, and
the first four design-contract corrections (C2, C6, T9, and the one time-value
role C5 defines for the rest of the phase to adopt).
</objective>
