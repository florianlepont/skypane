<objective>
Four controls exist today for two settings: the Frame strip (an immediate POST),
the "Screen on / off" and "Quiet hours" form groups (applied on Save), and the
nav reminder repeating both a third time — with three different sentences about
when a change reaches the frame, one of which ("within about 5 minutes") the
audit could not substantiate. Worse, the two form controls can contradict the
strip, and pressing a strip switch with unsaved edits raises the browser's
leave-page dialog for a change the strip is itself about to apply.

Give each setting one control and one stated delay. The strip owns Screen on/off
and Quiet hours on/off; the form keeps the quiet-hours schedule only; one computed
sentence, derived from plan 22-02's result, says when it lands.

Purpose: this is also the phase's highest-blast-radius regression risk. Removing
the checkboxes without changing the write path would switch the screen and quiet
hours off on EVERY settings save — the user-visible symptom being the frame going
dark after changing a theme (22-RESEARCH.md Pitfall 1, D-12.1). The markup change
and the write-path change are inseparable and land together, here.
Output: one control per setting, one sentence, and two pinned guards against the
regressions the audit alone would have shipped.
</objective>
