<objective>
Wire OTA into the wake: classify the previous attempt at boot, apply an offer before the hash-skip (battery refusal, UPDATING screen, download, restart or fail with backoff), confirm a trial image before the deep sleep of its first successful wake, and extend the wake budget. Record every firmware change of the phase in VENDOR.md.

Purpose: this is where the phase's highest-risk ordering lives (research Pitfall 1): confirm before sleeping, in the same wake. It is also where D-12, D-14 and D-15 become device behaviour.
Output: wired state machine and dispatcher, budget constants and asserts, VENDOR.md.
</objective>
