<objective>
Build the reproducible per-wake energy model tool that fits when the data can separate the unknowns and refuses when it cannot.

Purpose: WAKE-02. The research showed the old-pack aggregates give a non-physical three-parameter solve (negative coefficients). A tool that always returns a figure would repeat that mistake; a tool that refuses merely because the standing draw is near zero (which the old-pack evidence says it is, at most about 1 mA) would never produce a result. Success for this plan is a committed, tested tool that, run on the baseline, either prints a fit with its ranges or refuses with the exact missing data named.
Output: hardware/wakemodel.py, tests, fixtures, committed reproducible output, the model section in hardware/WAKE-ENERGY.md.
</objective>

  <name>Task 1: Join, classify and the identifiability check</name>
  <files>hardware/wakemodel.py, hardware/test_wakemodel.py, hardware/fixtures/wakemodel-synthetic.jsonl, hardware/fixtures/wakemodel-collinear.jsonl</files>
  <behavior>
    - Join: a row's prev_* fields describe the wake with wake_seq equal to this row's prev_seq; a sequence gap is reported as missed wakes and those wakes counted as unknown, not guessed
    - Classify from prev_redraw and prev_outcome: 0 and ok is no-change, 1 is redraw, 2 is deferred, any other outcome is failed; rows with null wake fields are unknown
    - Segments: after ignoring the first 24 h, consecutive segments of a configurable length (default 48 h; 24 h segments carry a voltage fall inside the ADC and curve noise at hourly cadence, so 48 h is the default and a baseline window of at least 14 days gives six segments) each give charge used (from the rated curve through the logtools conversion, median edge points, quiet-hour polls excluded as logtools does), hours elapsed, count of no-change wakes and count of redraw wakes
    - Solve: the three unknowns (standing draw in mA, no-change wake energy and redraw wake energy in mAh) are fitted by non-negative least squares, done exactly by solving every non-empty subset of the three as an ordinary least-squares problem with the others held at zero and keeping the feasible (all coefficients not below zero) solution of least residual, ties to the fewer parameters; 95 percent intervals come from the unconstrained inverse normal matrix and a t quantile table and are labelled approximate; negative lower ends are clipped at zero
    - Identifiability: the tool computes the design matrix of hours, no-change count and redraw count per segment and refuses when there are fewer than 6 segments, when the redraw share of segments varies by less than a stated spread (default 0.15), when the scaled design condition number exceeds a stated limit (default 100), or when the redraw wake energy is not resolved (the non-negative fit sets it to zero or its 95 percent interval touches zero); each refusal names its reason and names what data would separate the unknowns (for example a window at another interval, so that hours and wake counts stop being proportional, or a period with a different redraw share)
    - Near-zero standing draw: the standing draw is always printed as an upper bound (the upper end of its 95 percent interval, floor zero) with the non-negative point value shown only as "best fit, may be zero"; the no-change wake energy is printed with its range when its interval excludes zero and as an upper bound otherwise; neither is a refusal
    - Synthetic fixture with known standing draw, no-change energy and redraw energy and varied redraw share and a second cadence across segments: recovered within 10 percent, ranges contain the truth
    - Synthetic fixture whose true standing draw is zero (and one with 0.05 mA): the fit is returned, the standing draw bound is at or above the truth, no negative value is printed anywhere
    - Collinear fixture (same redraw share in every segment, one cadence): refuses with the collinearity or low-spread reason and prints no energy figure
    - A wake with no p_ block (first after an OTA) and a seq gap are counted as unknown wakes in the segment, with no effect on the other counts
    - The committed old-pack export of 2026-11-03 (no per-wake fields): refuses with "no per-wake telemetry in this export". Run the tool on it once under the final rules; if it refuses for any other reason, use a synthetic export without per-wake fields for this test instead
  </behavior>
  <action>

Solve the three-unknown non-negative least squares by hand (normal equations with Gaussian elimination and column scaling, over every subset of the unknowns as the behavior block says), compute residual standard error and 95 percent intervals from the inverse normal matrix with a t quantile table for small degrees of freedom held in the tool, and apply every refusal rule of the behavior block.
