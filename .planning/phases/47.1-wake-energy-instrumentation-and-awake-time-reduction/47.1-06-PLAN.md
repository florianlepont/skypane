  <name>Task 1: Join, classify and the identifiability check</name>
  <files>hardware/wakemodel.py, hardware/test_wakemodel.py, hardware/fixtures/wakemodel-synthetic.jsonl, hardware/fixtures/wakemodel-collinear.jsonl</files>
  <behavior>
    - Join: a row's prev_* fields describe the wake with wake_seq equal to this row's prev_seq; a sequence gap is reported as missed wakes and those wakes counted as unknown, not guessed
    - Classify from prev_redraw and prev_outcome: 0 and ok is no-change, 1 is redraw, 2 is deferred, any other outcome is failed; rows with null wake fields are unknown
    - Segments: after ignoring the first 24 h, consecutive segments of a configurable length (default 48 h) each give charge used (from the rated curve through the logtools conversion, median edge points, quiet-hour polls excluded as logtools does), hours elapsed, count of no-change wakes and count of redraw wakes
    - Identifiability: the tool computes the design matrix of hours, no-change count and redraw count per segment and refuses when there are fewer than 6 segments, when the redraw share of segments varies by less than a stated spread (default 0.15), when the scaled design condition number exceeds a stated limit (default 100), when the solve gives any negative point estimate, or when any 95 percent interval touches zero; each refusal names its reason
    - Synthetic fixture with known standing draw, no-change energy and redraw energy and varied redraw share across segments: recovered within 10 percent, ranges contain the truth
    - Collinear fixture (same redraw share in every segment): refuses with the collinearity reason and prints no energy figure
    - The committed old-pack export of 2026-11-03 (no per-wake fields): refuses with "no per-wake telemetry in this export"
  </behavior>
  <action>
Create hardware/wakemodel.py (stdlib only, Python 3 compatible with the 3.11 venv and 3.14 CI, no new dependency, not in the mypy list, loaded the way test_logtools_model.py loads logtools). Load hardware/logtools.py by path with importlib and reuse its curve conversion, endpoint selection and refusal of the uncalibrated band rather than re-deriving a curve; do not edit logtools.py. Solve the three-unknown least squares by hand (normal equations with Gaussian elimination and column scaling), compute residual standard error and 95 percent intervals from the inverse normal matrix with a t quantile table for small degrees of freedom held in the tool, and apply every refusal rule of the behavior block. Estimation is labelled as energy per wake in mAh and a standing draw in mA derived from voltage and timing on a rated capacity: never a current measurement. As a physical sanity check, also print for each wake class the device-reported mean awake time (direct measurement from prev_awake_ms, request, download, draw, SPI) and the implied mean current during awake time (energy divided by awake hours); flag it as implausible above 500 mA for the no-change class and above 200 mA for the redraw class, in which case the result is demoted to a refusal because the model conflicts with the timing (these two limits are stated assumptions in the output, adjustable by flag). Add synthetic fixtures built by a documented generator inside the test (the fixture files are written by a small deterministic builder called from the test and committed, so the truth values are in the test), and the refusal cases. Tests assert returned values and CLI stdout and exit codes only (exit 0 for a fit, exit 2 for a refusal), never source text.
