<task type="auto" tdd="true">
  <name>Task 1: Breakdown and gap-versus-awake subcommands</name>
  <files>hardware/wakemodel.py, hardware/test_wakemodel_breakdown.py</files>
  <behavior>
    - breakdown prints, per wake class, the count, median and 90th percentile of each device-reported duration, the Wi-Fi attempt distribution and the share of wakes per class per configured cadence group
    - The classes come from device fields only; wakes with null fields are counted as unknown and never added to a class
    - gap-vs-awake takes the configured sleep_s and prints, for consecutive rows joined by sequence number, the gap minus sleep_s next to the device p_aw of the earlier wake, with median and 90th percentile of the difference, and states the unexplained difference (gap minus sleep_s minus p_aw) which includes timer error and the boot time before the app clock starts; it does not interpret that difference as either
    - Quiet-hour gaps and sequence gaps are set aside and counted, as logtools checkin-gaps does
    - A synthetic export with known durations gives the known medians
  </behavior>
  <action>
Extend hardware/wakemodel.py with the two subcommands (stdlib only, functions kept small, same loaders as the fit). Tests use synthetic exports built in tmp_path and assert stdout and values, never source text. Wording rule: every printed duration is labelled device-reported; any energy-like value is labelled an estimate and is only printed by the fit.
  </action>
  <verify>
    <automated>server/.venv/bin/python3 -m pytest hardware/test_wakemodel.py hardware/test_wakemodel_breakdown.py -q --no-cov -n0</automated>

  <done>Breakdown and gap-vs-awake behave on synthetic data with known answers; earlier fit tests still pass.</done>
