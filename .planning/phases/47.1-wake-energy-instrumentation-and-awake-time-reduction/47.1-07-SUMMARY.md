## Decisions made

- Wake classes: `p_rd=1` is redraw, `p_rd=2` deferred, `p_rd=0` with outcome `ok` no-change, `p_rd=0` with another outcome failed (literal reading of the plan). Deferred, failed and unknown wakes are "unmodelled"; a segment with more than 25 percent of them is dropped and reported (default, flag-adjustable).
- A segment is used only when complete (its end is not after the last row), so exactly 14 days of data at an interval gives six 48 h segments and one day less gives five.
- Intervals are centred on the non-negative point value, with standard errors from the unconstrained fit, lower end clipped at zero.
- Segments starting above 4000 mV are dropped with the reason (the tool does not claim the uncalibrated band).

## Deviations from Plan

**1. Synthetic pack rating is 1000 mAh, not 3000.** With 3000 mAh, whole-millivolt quantisation was too large against each 48 h fall and recovery missed 10 percent (standing draw 0.80 against a truth of 0.60). 1000 mAh is a documented synthetic rating chosen so quantisation is small; truths are 0.60 mA, 0.20 mAh, 1.20 mAh. Recovery on the committed fixture: 0.596 mA, 0.193 mAh, 1.236 mAh (all within 10 percent). This says nothing about real packs; real data has more noise than this fixture.

**2. Range containment is tested statistically.** On the fixture itself the redraw range (1.204 to 1.269) just misses the truth 1.20. The ranges are approximate 95 percent ranges, so the test requires recovery within 10 percent in every one of four shifted-start scenarios and the truth inside the range in at least 75 percent of the twelve (scenario, parameter) pairs, rather than forcing containment on one export. This means the printed ranges can be slightly narrow; they ignore curve and capacity error as the output states.

**3. Boundary fixtures use mixed intervals.** A constant-interval 14-day export is exactly collinear (hours proportional to wake count), so the boundary test uses alternating 60 and 30 minute intervals, not a pure hourly cadence. A constant-interval export is tested separately and refuses as ill-conditioned.

**4. TDD cadence:** tests and implementation were committed together (no separate RED commit). The committed output JSON and its reproduction test are in Task 1's commit, not Task 2's.

**5. Calibration of the fit limits was done only on synthetic data.** The default condition limit (100), share spread (0.15) and implied-current limits (500 and 200 mA) are the plan's values; none has been checked against a real baseline.
