<objective>
Close A-22 (D-04) and A-23 (D-05) on the Health page: stop the sparkline from
turning a 15 mV wiggle into a cliff, stop tuning the dot-suppression rule to one
measured canvas width, derive device staleness from the device's own wake
cadence, demote a single battery dip from a page-level error to a warning, and
fold the two signals that were excluded from `overall_severity` back into it.

Purpose: today Health calls a healthy 30-second-cadence device "stale" only after
an hour and an "outage" only after six, while simultaneously escalating one
100 mV reading dip to a page-level error and ignoring a real coverage gap and a
real source outage in the page's own severity. All four are miscalibrations, and
they are the reason the page's colour does not mean what a reader assumes.
Output: a new shared `companion/wake.py`, a fixed-range width-aware sparkline,
and a severity model whose inputs match what the page actually shows.
</objective>
