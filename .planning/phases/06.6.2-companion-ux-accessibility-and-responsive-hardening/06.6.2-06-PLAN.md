<objective>
Close UXA-14: derive a real `ok`/`warn`/`error` severity from Health's four existing D-14 signals instead of collapsing every non-healthy state into the same red/error treatment, and drive the sidebar nav dot, the mobile-nav dot, and the anomaly banner from that one severity so they can never disagree. Alongside that, close the remaining UXA-07 scope this phase owns: `flash_banner()`/`anomaly_banner()` carry the correct `role="status"`/`role="alert"` for their real outcome, chosen by severity rather than uniformly.

Purpose: the audit's own reproduced scenario is exact — Device and Pipeline fresh/green, only a warning-level corroboration disagreement, and the nav still shows a red dot and a red "Something needs attention" banner. That is a real loss of meaning: once any anomaly exists today, warning and error become visually indistinguishable at the two most prominent global signals.
Output: `health_page.overall_severity()`/`health_severity()`, a severity-aware `layout._health_alert_markup()`/`anomaly_banner()`/`flash_banner()`, and `ctx["health_severity"]`/`ctx["flash_role"]` threaded through every page render.
</objective>
