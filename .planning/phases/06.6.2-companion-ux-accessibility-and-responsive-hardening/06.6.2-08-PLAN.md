  <what-built>
Seven plans of accessibility/UX/design-token hardening across the companion web app: reworked colour tokens and a WCAG contrast checker with the light-theme accent darkened for AA compliance (plan 01); a server-side poll-concurrency lock with a "Polling…" button affordance (plan 02); guaranteed no-horizontal-overflow History containment and a genuinely accessible closed mobile-nav state that still works with JavaScript disabled (plan 03); a shared page-header component and distinct page-title typography across all five pages (plan 04); a refined sidebar with active-state pill, per-tab icons, sticky positioning, a footer region grouping the theme picker with a new POST-only Sign out control, plus a skip link and local favicon (plan 05); severity-aware (warning vs. error) nav dot and anomaly banner instead of one-size-fits-all red, plus role-correct flash banners (plan 06); and a dedicated, nav-free login shell with a strictly allowlisted deep-link return, autocomplete, and error focus/role handling (plan 07).
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
Run the companion service locally against a scratch state directory seeded with representative data (device health readings, a corroboration disagreement, a stale-pipeline fixture, and 3+ History rows — reuse this codebase's existing test-fixture seeding helpers rather than hand-writing new ones), then in a real local browser:

1. **Viewport matrix.** At 375px, 768px, 959px, 960px, and 1440px widths, with History populated: confirm no document-level horizontal scrollbar appears at any width (UXA-01).
2. **Mobile nav accessibility.** At 390px width, open DevTools' Accessibility/Elements panel with the hamburger menu closed: confirm none of the five nav links or three theme buttons appears in the accessibility tree. Tab from the hamburger toggle and confirm focus lands in main content, not inside the panel. Open the menu and confirm all links/buttons become focusable in order; press Escape and confirm focus returns to the toggle (UXA-02). Then disable JavaScript entirely and reload: confirm the menu panel renders fully visible and every link is reachable with no clipping (UXA-12).
3. **Contrast.** Visually spot-check the active sidebar link, primary button labels, and links in both Light and Dark themes against the new darker accent — nothing should look washed out or hard to read (UXA-04; the mechanical proof already lives in `companion/test_companion_app.py`'s token-contrast regression suite from plan 01).
4. **Sidebar.** Confirm the active nav link shows a tinted pill background (not just a thin line), each label has a small icon, the sidebar stays visible while scrolling a long Health page, and the footer region (theme picker + Sign out) sits visually separated at the bottom (D-17/D-21).
5. **Logout.** Click Sign out from both the desktop sidebar and the mobile dropdown; confirm it ends the session and returns to login. Confirm a bare `GET /logout` (typed directly in the address bar) no longer logs you out.
6. **Login deep link.** While logged out, visit `/health` directly; confirm you land on login, then after signing in you land back on `/health` (not always Config).
7. **Severity.** Seed a corroboration-disagreement-only fixture (device/pipeline both healthy) and confirm the Health nav dot and its banner show *warning* styling, not the red/error treatment; then seed a stale-device fixture and confirm it does show error/red.
8. **Poll lock.** Open two browser tabs on Config, click Trigger Poll Now in both as close together as practical; confirm only one actually runs a poll cycle and the other shows the "already in progress" message, and confirm the clicked button visibly disables/relabels immediately in the tab that succeeded.
9. **Keyboard-only pass.** Using only Tab/Shift+Tab/Enter/Escape, confirm the skip link is the very first focus stop and jumps to main content, and that every control this phase touched (nav, sidebar footer, login form, poll button) remains fully operable.

This is a local pass only — do not perform this against the production deployment; the full authenticated production walkthrough is Phase 06.6.3's closing responsibility.
