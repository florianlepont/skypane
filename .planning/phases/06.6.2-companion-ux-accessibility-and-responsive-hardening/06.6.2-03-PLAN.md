Add a comment above the new `body { overflow-x: hidden; }` line recording that this is UXA-01's guaranteed fix (verified by the audit's own real-browser measurement at 1440x900 with populated History data), and that `.data-table-wrap`'s internal `overflow-x: auto` is what still gives a genuinely wide table its own discoverable scroll — do not remove either half.
  </action>
