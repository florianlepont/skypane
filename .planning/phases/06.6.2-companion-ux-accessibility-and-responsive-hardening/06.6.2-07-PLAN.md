<objective>
Close the remaining UXA-03 scope this phase owns: replace the login page's reuse of the full authenticated `page_shell()` (which today renders the complete sidebar, every nav tab, and the full theme selector before the user has signed in) with a dedicated, minimal `login_shell()`; preserve the originally-requested protected route through an authentication round-trip via a strictly allowlisted `next` parameter; and finish login's own accessibility gaps — `autocomplete="current-password"`, `role="alert"` + focus on the password field for error/lockout states.

Purpose: today, opening any protected URL without a session redirects to bare `/login` with no memory of what was requested, and renders a page that visually implies the whole site's navigation is usable before authentication — a real, reproducible defect the audit found on the live production login surface.
Output: `layout.login_shell()`, `app._validated_next_route()`, a rewritten login GET/POST flow, and `.login-shell`/`.login-card` CSS.
</objective>
