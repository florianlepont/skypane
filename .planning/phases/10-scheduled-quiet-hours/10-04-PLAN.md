<objective>
Gate `server/poll_loop.py`'s render pipeline on the quiet-hours window: draw the
QUIET HOURS screen once at window entry (D-05), hold silently for the rest of the window,
and resume the live board on the first cycle after it ends with no transition screen (D-07).

Purpose: `poll_loop.py` is the only process that renders and writes `panel.bin`.
`byos_server.py` (plan 10-03) decides how long the device sleeps; this plan decides what
the device finds on the panel when it wakes at the far side of the window, and what it
was left looking at when it went to sleep.

Output: an early-return quiet-hours branch near the top of `run_once()`, a
`poll_state["quiet_hours_active"]` flag, a window-exit repaint that cannot leave a stale
QUIET HOURS image on the glass, and extended coverage in `server/test_poll_loop.py`.
</objective>
