<objective>
Make the device sleep through the quiet-hours window by extending the `sleep_s` the
vendored BYOS server hands back on `GET /device/v1/display` (D-01).

Purpose: this is the whole battery win. Extending `sleep_s` skips the wake + Wi-Fi +
HTTPS round-trip entirely for the length of the window, not just the e-ink refresh — and
it needs zero firmware change, because `sleep_s` is already a fully server-controlled,
per-response value the firmware consumes as-is.

Output: `read_quiet_hours()` and `quiet_hours_sleep_s()` inside
`stub-server/byos_server.py`, a byte-for-byte duplicate of
`server/device_config.py`'s window arithmetic (the vendor boundary forbids importing it),
a `stub-server/VENDOR.md` local-modification entry, and extended coverage in
`stub-server/test_poll_cycle.py` including an automated drift guard between the two copies.
</objective>
