<objective>
`companion/test_browser_ux.py` is 26 checks, the slowest file in the suite at
roughly 48 seconds, and the only harness in this repo that can see behaviour
rather than strings. Phase 23 adds five new controls, a ticking counter, two
self-refreshing pages and motion on eight surfaces — every one of which needs this
file, and three of which cannot be verified anywhere else. Before that queue
starts, three things it lacks get built: a no-JS helper, a 360 px viewport
constant, and a motion-neutral context for the sweep that forces every `<details>`
open.

plan's own finding and it is the sharpest of the three: the general disclosure
sweep opens every `<details>` on every route at three widths in two languages and
measures immediately. Today nothing animates, so "immediately" and "finally" are
the same moment. Plans 23-08 and 23-10 make them different moments, and a check
that measures a box mid-transition fails intermittently for a reason no one will
find quickly.
Output: three helpers, three existing checks rewritten onto them, and a check
count that has not moved.
</objective>
