<objective>
This app has fifteen `transition:` declarations, zero `@keyframes`, zero
`animation:` declarations and one global `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`
override that has covered every one of those transitions for free since 06.6.2.
Phase 23 is about to add motion to eight surfaces across nine plans. Before any of
them runs, the phase needs a vocabulary to spend from and a guard that fails when
a plan overspends.

Purpose: a "motion budget" on this codebase is not a document — it is a token set
plus an executable rule. `references/accessibility-contrast.md` already records the
standing verdict that a per-rule reduced-motion block is dead code, and
`references/control-density.md:78` already pre-approves D3's rotating chevron on
exactly that reasoning. What is missing is the enforcement: nothing today would
notice a second near-identical keyframes block, a hard-coded 400 ms duration, a
third reduce block, or `interpolate-size` arriving from a blog post.
Output: two duration tokens, one shared `@keyframes skypane-pulse`, one named
comment recording the view-transition gap, and one harness check that every later
plan in this phase must keep green.
</objective>

Binding constraints:
- Two duration tokens, no more. "Zero new custom properties" has been a stated
  achievement of Phases 20 and 21; breaking it must be deliberate and minimal.
  `--motion-fast` and `--motion-slow` are the whole budget. A plan that needs a
  third duration states why in its own SUMMARY or reuses one of these two.
- ONE `@keyframes`. D14's breathing dot and D22's pulse are the same animation;
  `23-RESEARCH.md` names "a single shared `@keyframes skypane-pulse` reused by
  both" as the right shape and "two near-identical keyframe blocks" as the wrong
  one. It animates `opacity` only — never `transform: scale()` on a dot, which
  would repaint a layout box sixty times a second for a 6 px element.
- The keyframes is DEFINED here and CONSUMED by later plans. This plan declares no
  `animation:` on any selector: the guard below asserts every animation reference
  resolves, so defining an unused keyframes is safe and referencing an undefined
  one is not.
- `interpolate-size` / `calc-size()` are Chromium-only and Baseline limited. They
  are banned by the guard, not by a comment. `grid-template-rows: 0fr -> 1fr` is
  the sanctioned height-animation mechanism, and it is plan 23-08's/23-10's to use.
- Do not add a per-rule `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` block for a
  colour, border, shadow or transform transition. The global block covers it, and
  `references/accessibility-contrast.md:90` records the redundant block as dead
  code. The guard pins the count so this cannot happen by accident.
