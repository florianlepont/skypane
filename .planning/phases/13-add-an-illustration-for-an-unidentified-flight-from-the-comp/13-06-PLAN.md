<objective>
Wire the surface to the storage: widen the illustration membership set to a per-request union of static targets and server-persisted manual keys (D-09), thread three new ctx keys and eight new flash keys, and add the two state-changing routes — `POST /airlines/resolve` (Step A) and `POST /airlines/manual-resolutions/{prefix}/delete`.

The membership gate is widened for the *read* path and for Step B's *write* path, but only ever from already-persisted server state — never from the request. Output: one widened helper and its two call sites, three ctx keys, eight flash keys with messages and ARIA roles, two POST handlers, and a documentation update to the page-module contract.
