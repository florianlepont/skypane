  <behavior>
    - spacing-replay takes an export, a list of minimum redraw spacings in minutes (default 0, 10, 30, 60, 120) and the configured interval, and prints for each spacing: redraw wakes that would be suppressed as an upper bound (a redraw wake is suppressed when fewer than that many minutes passed since the last redraw that was allowed), the share of redraw wakes this is, the worst-case image age in minutes (spacing plus interval plus the median awake time), and the device-reported awake seconds that those suppressed redraws used (sum of download and draw time, labelled device-reported)
    - Spacing 0 suppresses nothing and the worst-case age equals the interval plus the median awake time
    - A synthetic export with redraws every 5 minutes and a spacing of 30 suppresses five of every six
    - Output states that the count is an upper bound because the export does not record whether the image had changed, and that no energy figure is derived
