  <action>
Append a "Reduction selection" section to hardware/WAKE-ENERGY.md. For each candidate state: what it changes, the baseline quantity that selects it, the default gate, the number from the baseline, PASS or FAIL against the gate, the expected effect with its basis, and the risk. Default gates (the owner may change a threshold at the decision):
- Compress the image download (server variant plus firmware inflate): median p_dl of redraw wakes at least 3000 ms, AND the production-image compression check of plan 11 shows every shipped theme at 0.5 or less of the raw size. Expected effect: radio-on seconds saved are about median p_dl times one minus the ratio; the preview-render ratio of about 0.024 is a render of preview states only (assumption A8, to be re-measured on production themes in plan 11), not a promise.
- Light sleep or a lower CPU clock during the panel busy-wait: median redraw wake spends at least 20 s waiting on the panel (p_dr minus p_sp). This candidate does not shorten awake time; its effect is on energy, whose size depends on idle current versus the panel's own draw, which no document gives (assumption A7), so it is built as an experiment: it ships only if the before and after comparison on the real frame shows a difference beyond the spreads; otherwise it is reported as not demonstrated.
- Cache the Wi-Fi channel and access point in RTC memory (with a full-scan fallback): median wf at least 1500 ms, or 90th percentile at least 3000 ms, or more than one attempt in at least 5 percent of wakes. Relevant on every wake class, and at long intervals where nearly every wake redraws it is a small share of a redraw wake, which the table states with the number.
- Static IP (provisioning cable step, no release): median wf at least 1000 ms; needs the owner to do a USB provisioning step and a router reservation outside the DHCP pool, and has no DHCP fallback in the code. The DHCP share of wf is not separable from the baseline, so the expected saving is the Phase 34 estimate (about 0.7 s of 1.3 to 1.7 s), unmeasured.
Exclusions with reasons: shrinking the per-row SPI pacing (risk of a corrupted colour refresh, changes what is on glass; lowest priority; out of this phase), moving the NVS commits (tens of milliseconds, unmeasured), and everything already done (hash skip, TLS resume, keep-alive, DHCP restore, rail only while drawing). State the measured quantity (device-reported seconds per redraw wake: total awake, radio-on, panel wait), say energy is claimed only where the model fit resolved it and is otherwise recorded as not measured, and fix the claim rule: same cadence, same firmware otherwise, at least 30 redraw wakes before and after, medians and 90th percentiles from the telemetry, a reduction is claimed only when the after 90th percentile of the claimed quantity is below the before median, otherwise "not demonstrated". Every selected candidate must keep the image shown identical (byte-identical decoded image, same hash), the hash-skip, quiet hours, display-off and battery-critical behaviour, proven by the existing suites staying green and unchanged.
