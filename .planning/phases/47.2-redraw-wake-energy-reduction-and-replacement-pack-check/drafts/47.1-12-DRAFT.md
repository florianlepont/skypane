Build the panel-wait experiment: the CPU idles at full clock for about 27 s of every redraw (no power-management configuration exists today). Reduce what the CPU does during that wait without touching the panel sequence.

Output: pure slice-planning module with host tests, the guarded change in the busy wait, build.
</objective>
