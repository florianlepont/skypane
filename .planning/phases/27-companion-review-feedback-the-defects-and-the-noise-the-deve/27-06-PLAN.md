<objective>
Two corrections that are both about the same thing: a settings page that says too
much, in too many shapes.

**Correction 2 — one title form.** Titles sit inside the setting tile on some cards
and above it on others. The developer noticed because inconsistency is what the eye
finds first on a page it is trying to skim.

**Correction 4 — cut the explanatory text.** *"beaucoup trop de texte
d'explication"* on the wake-interval slider, on its two gauges, and in the Quiet
hours card's paragraph.

These are one plan because they are one edit pass over the same file and because
they share one failure mode: **cutting or moving text that was carrying something.**
The gauges' wording is not decoration — it is D18's honesty contract, which refuses
to state a battery figure the device's own history cannot support. The Quiet hours
paragraph is explanation *plus* a delay sentence carrying live state. A cut that
takes either is not a cut, it is a regression.

And the title inventory must be **run, not read**. The provisional count is 8 titles
inside cards against 3 above — but the 3 may be *supersection* intros rather than
card titles, and a supersection intro is not the same object as a card title. If
that is what the inventory finds, the honest answer is that there is already one
title form for cards, and the plan says so instead of converting three things that
were never the problem.
</objective>
