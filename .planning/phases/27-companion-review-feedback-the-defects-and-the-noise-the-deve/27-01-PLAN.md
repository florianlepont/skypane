<objective>
D17's quiet-hours dial shipped broken with every one of its checks passing. The arc
was asserted correct **server-side for the saved value**. The handles were asserted
**to move**. The value was asserted **to persist to disk**. Three correct halves,
and the defect lived in the relationship none of them measured: after an
interaction, the arc said 23:00→07:00 while the fields said 08:00→18:00.

An arc that disagrees with the fields beside it is worse than no arc. It actively
lies.

This plan turns that lesson into an instrument. It writes **one helper whose whole
subject is agreement**: hand it several rendered surfaces and a way to decode each
back to a canonical value, and it asserts that the decoded set has exactly one
member — plus that that member is what the interaction asked for, and that it is
not what was there before.

It adds **zero net checks**. Its entire output is the sequence the later plans call
instead of hand-transcribing eleven agreement assertions, each with its own chance
to assert two endpoints and call it a relationship.

It also adds the one decoder this phase cannot do without and which nothing in the
harness can do today: **reading an SVG's resolved geometry back to a number.** The
arc's correctness is currently only checkable as server-rendered HTML; after this
phase it is a live, computed value, and a check that cannot read the computed value
cannot see the defect.
</objective>
