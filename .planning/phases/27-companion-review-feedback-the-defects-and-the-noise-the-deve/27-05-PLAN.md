<objective>
The developer's words: *"Je comprends pas l'intérêt de ces cartes des pistes, elles
représentent la même chose que mes schémas."*

D16's drawn map of Orly cost a whole wave in Phase 25 and teaches the developer
nothing his own schematics did not already. It comes out. The three native radios
return to being the control — which is to say they go back to being *visibly* the
control, because they never stopped being the control underneath.

**That is why this removal is safe, and the reason is worth stating precisely: it is
CFG-47's own strongest clause.** The requirement insisted the three strips *be* the
same native radios, so that selection, arrow-key navigation and saving all worked
with scripts blocked. They were. The map was a drawing wrapped around a working
control. Remove the drawing and the control is untouched — no state migration, no
new control, no re-derivation of how a runway is chosen.

**CFG-47 is already retired**, in place, with its reason, in three places in
`.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md`: the ticked row (which keeps its `[x]`, because the work
really was done), the traceability ledger row, and the D16 section. Met-as-worded
then withdrawn-as-a-product-decision is a different fact from "not met" and the
record says so. This plan's job with respect to that record is to **verify it and
leave it alone**.
</objective>
