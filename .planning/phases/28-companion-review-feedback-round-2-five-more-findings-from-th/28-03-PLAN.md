<objective>
The developer's words: *"Le curseur des heures calmes fonctionne mieux mais est toujours
bugué."*

27-02 genuinely fixed the cross-surface agreement bug — the arc and the caption DO
follow the handles now, under drag, keyboard, typed field and preset, including midnight
wraparound. What the developer is seeing is the limitation 27-02 documented in
`quiet_dial_readout_html()`'s own docstring and shipped anyway:

> `"23:00 → 07:00 · 8h"` becomes `"300 → 420 · "` after ANY interaction, and never
> recovers without a full page reload.

Two separate design decisions produce those two halves, and this plan inverts both
deliberately and in writing:

1. **The endpoints.** `paintReadouts()` writes `readoutQuantity()`'s output — a raw
   minute-of-day number. The HH:MM codec `numberToField()` already exists in the same
   file, but it is wired only to the native `<input type="time">`, never to a readout.
2. **The duration.** The duration span's readout carries `data-value-readout-text=""` —
   an EMPTY template, on purpose, so `paintReadouts()` always blanks it. That was the
   honest choice at the time (a blank duration is better than a stale lying one), and it
   is the thing the developer is now calling "still buggy."

**The hard constraint on the duration is not the arithmetic, it is the language.**
`layout.duration_text()` over `_age_bucket()` is this app's ONE length-of-time ladder,
and `value-controls.js` writes no copy. There is a shipped precedent for exactly this
shape: `relative-time.js` rewrites `<time>` elements once a second in the reader's own
language, and carries **no French at all**, because `layout.py` renders the four bucket
wordings (`"#m ago"`, `"#h ago"`, …) onto the page as translated attributes with `#`
marking the quantity's place. This plan spends that same mechanism a second time rather
than porting a ladder into JavaScript.

**Out of scope:** Bug B (the handle's `:active` collapse) landed in 28-02, in different
files. 27-02's pair seam — `_assert_surfaces_agree`, `_quiet_arc_minutes`,
`_fraction_pair_minutes`, the server-authoritative arc — is verified correct and is not
touched.
</objective>
