<objective>
26-06 built the emitter and proved it changes nothing. This plan is the change:
six empty states stop being two grey sentences and start telling the user what to
do next.

The work is small and the ways to get it wrong are specific and known. Two of the
six sit inside stat tiles and must stay compact. At least one will want to point
the user at the page they are already looking at. And an empty state's "next
action" is the one place in this app where it is easy to invent a route that does
not exist, because nobody is looking at an empty page carefully.
