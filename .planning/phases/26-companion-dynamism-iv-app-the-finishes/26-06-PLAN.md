`empty_state()` an illustration and a next action. The trap: the fastest way to
draw a small line illustration is to type an `<svg>` with a stroke colour in it,

dark mode, and quietly becomes this codebase's second drawing path three weeks
after Phase 24 created the first one specifically to prevent that.

This plan does the work through `companion/draw.py`, keeps the existing six
callers byte-identical, and proves both.
