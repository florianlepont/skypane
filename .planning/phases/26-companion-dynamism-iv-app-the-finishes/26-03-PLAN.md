<objective>
26-01 left a dialog that opens and closes over a list of every destination in the
app. This plan makes it a palette: type, see fewer things, choose one — and does
it without acquiring any of the four defects that palettes reliably ship with.

Three of the four are solved by refusing to write code: `<dialog>` +
`showModal()` already traps focus, already restores it, already closes on Escape.
The fourth — announcing results without machine-gunning a screen reader — is the
one this codebase has already paid tuition on, in Phase 23, with three switches.
A palette types on every keystroke, so it is the worst possible place to repeat
that lesson, and this plan spends its accessibility budget there.
</objective>

<decisions_covered>
CFG-54. The filtering is deliberately plain substring matching over the visible
label and its group name (`indexOf`, case-folded), not fuzzy ranking: the ES5
subset is mandatory, there is no dependency budget, and 23-RESEARCH.md already
judged that `String.prototype.indexOf` over a server-rendered index is the right
answer at this size. With six destinations plus per-page sections, ranking would
be solving a problem the data does not have.
</decisions_covered>
