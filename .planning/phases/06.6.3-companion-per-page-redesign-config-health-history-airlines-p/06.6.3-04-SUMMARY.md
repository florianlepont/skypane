## Decisions Made
- Separated the anomaly banner's category text from the `ANOMALY_BANNER_TEXT` fallback tail with an em dash rather than a period, specifically to avoid silently reconstructing `collect_anomalies()`'s own exact literal string (which a pre-existing pinned regression check asserts must never render on the page) — see Deviations for the concrete collision this avoided.
- Threaded `collect_anomalies()`'s output through `compute_health_state()`'s returned dict (a new `"anomalies"` key) rather than having `render()` recompute it a second time — extends WR-04's existing single-computation discipline so the nav-tab dot and the on-page banner's category naming can never structurally disagree.
- Kept `ANOMALY_BANNER_TEXT` as a trailing fallback tail in the rewritten banner text (the plan's own action text explicitly permits this) rather than removing it outright — this is what let every one of the ~8 pre-existing `ANOMALY_BANNER_TEXT in rendered` / `.count(...)` presence/absence checks in `test_status_pages.py` keep passing unmodified.

- **Committed in:** `d3e83b0` (Task 1 commit)
