<objective>
Decide, on measured evidence, whether to take the optional third extraction — and
close the phase with an explicit verdict on D-05's gate.

RESEARCH.md ranks Flights third: the smallest of the three candidate groups, but
the one with zero coupling found anywhere, making it the cheapest remaining win
if one is needed. Its own Open Question 1 says plainly that whether groups 1 and
2 alone clear D-05's bar "can only be measured by actually running the split",
and recommends the third group be made conditional on exactly that measurement.
This plan is that conditional.

**Correction to RESEARCH.md that this plan must handle.** RESEARCH.md scopes the
early Flights cluster as pre-plan-01 lines 3689-3959. That range contains four
`check(...)` calls, not three: the second of them is
`_the_quiet_schedule_link_meets_the_hit_target_floor_at_360px`, a quiet-hours
caption check that visits both Home and Display and has nothing to do with
Flights. It sits physically between the first and second Flights checks. So the
early cluster is not contiguous, the extraction must be anchored on check names
rather than on a line range, and that one check must be left behind. Group size
is 8 checks — 3 early plus 5 late — which matches RESEARCH.md's total even though
its early sub-count was reached a different way.

Purpose: honour D-04's "2-3 groups" upper bound with evidence rather than
appetite, and give D-05 the conclusion it was written to produce.
Output: either a fourth harness at 8/8 with the parent at 68/68, or a recorded
decision not to extract — plus, in both cases, the D-05 verdict and the follow-up
recommendation.
