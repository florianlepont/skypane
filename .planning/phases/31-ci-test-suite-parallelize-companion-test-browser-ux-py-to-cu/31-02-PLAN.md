<objective>
Extract the SVG-drawing scenario group — the complete 24-04 battery ring / 24-06
day band / 24-07 regularity grid / 24-08 hero series — out of
`companion/test_browser_ux.py` into its own independently-runnable harness.

This is the first of D-04's two mandatory extractions and RESEARCH.md's rank-1
candidate: it has the cleanest boundary in the file (three of its checks already
spin up their own isolated `Harness()` rather than reusing the shared one, so the
original authors already treated this cluster as isolation-worthy), and its three
`_in_both_themes()` loops make its real wall-time share larger than its
line-count share suggests.

**Boundary correction against RESEARCH.md.** RESEARCH.md scopes this group at 9
checks (pre-plan-01 lines 9411-10882) and records "Coupling out: None found".
That is wrong in one specific way, verified against the live file:
`_band_harness()` is defined inside the day-band block and is called from FOUR
sites — two inside RESEARCH.md's range, and two in the 24-08 hero checks that
RESEARCH.md leaves behind. Cutting at 10882 strands the hero checks with a
`NameError`. Widening the cut to include the 24-08 hero block fixes it and is the
better boundary anyway: the widened block is contiguous (no non-drawings check
sits between the first and last), it is one coherent plan series, and it makes
`_band_harness`, `_grid_harness`, `BAND_SEED_PARIS_HOURS`, `RING_PAGES`,
`RING_VALUE`, `RING_TRACK` and `_band_rgba` have exactly zero references outside
the moved block. The group is therefore **11 checks**, not 9.

Purpose: D-04's incremental decomposition, and the larger of the two guaranteed
wall-time wins.
Output: `companion/test_browser_ux_health_drawings.py` at 11/11, and
`companion/test_browser_ux.py` reduced to 85/85.
