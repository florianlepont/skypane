<objective>
Quiet hours are currently two time fields and three preset buttons. That is a
correct control and a poor picture: nothing on the page shows you that 23:00 to
07:00 is eight hours, or that it covers the part of the day you are asleep.

D17 draws the day as a ring and lets you grab the ends of the quiet window.

Three things make this the most delicate plan in the phase, and each is a
constraint rather than a feature:

1. **The time inputs stay.** The audit's "hidden synced `<input type=\"time\">`"
   assumed the dial replaces them. It must not. A native time input is the only
   control on this card a user can *type* into, and typing 23:00 beats dragging to
   it every time. The dial is an addition.
2. **B14 is already fixed and must survive.** 22-10 shipped the visible 24 h text
   beside each time input, because a browser in en-US renders the stored "23:00"
   as "11:00 PM" directly beside a preset button labelled "Night (23:00-07:00)" —
   one value, two notations, no way to tell they are the same. A dial that removes
   that sibling reopens a closed defect.
3. **The default window wraps midnight.** 23:00 to 07:00 is eight hours going
   forward through midnight, and sixteen going the other way. Arithmetic that
   assumes `end > start` draws the wrong arc, computes the wrong duration, and
   labels it confidently.

Output: a server-drawn ring that shows the saved window correctly even with
scripts blocked, two drag handles that appear only when they can work, and two
time inputs that are exactly as authoritative as they are today.
</objective>
