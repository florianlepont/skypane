<objective>
Display's Frame colours card shows eighteen theme chips, always expanded. Phase 22
already shrank them all to the compact density and recorded the outcome plainly:
the page-height target *"is NOT met and cannot be by density alone — folding the
grid behind the big preview is D5, Phase 23"*.

This is that fold. And because 22-10 wrote the shortfall down, this plan has an
unusually honest success criterion available to it: **a measured page height**,
before and after, at 390 px. Not "a carousel exists".

Three things make this the most dangerous plan in the phase:

1. **The one feature query.** The whole `.theme-chip` selected state — border,
   inset ring, accent wash, check glyph, hover restore, and the dashed no-`:has()`
   "Current" marker — lives inside the single `@supports selector(:has(*))` block,
   whose specificity arithmetic is explicitly marked "verified, not to be
   re-derived". A carousel that wants its own live "current slide" state is one
   careless `@supports` away from failing two named checks.
2. **The renderer is shared.** `_theme_chip_grid_html()` has four call sites
   (departures, arrivals, calendar, rule-add). Forking it for the carousel gives
   this app two chip renderers that will drift.
3. **The audit says "dialog" and it must not be one.** A `<dialog>` cannot open
   without script. Eighteen themes behind a dead control with scripts blocked is
   precisely the defect this phase exists to prevent.

Output: the departures grid presented as a scroll-snap carousel with a big live
preview, the full grid behind a native disclosure, one chip renderer still, one
feature query still, and a page-height number.
</objective>
