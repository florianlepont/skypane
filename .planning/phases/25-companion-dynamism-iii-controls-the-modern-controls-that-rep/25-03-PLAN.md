<objective>
Today the runway is chosen from three cards, each optionally carrying a 338-371 KB
photograph of a runway. The cards work — they are a native radiogroup and they
save with scripts blocked — but three photographs side by side do not tell you
where these runways *are* relative to each other, which is the one thing a map is
for.

D16 draws that map. The interesting part is what it must NOT change: the three
`<input type="radio" name="tracked_runway" class="visually-hidden"
form="settings-form">` elements stay exactly as they are. They are the control.
The map is what the user looks at while operating them.

That is why this plan adds **no script at all** — the hardest thing about the
other four controls is absent here by construction. A radio group already has
arrow-key navigation, already has a native selected state, and already submits.

Output: one schematic SVG of Orly's three runways, each strip a selectable target
that meets its touch floor at 360 px, painted from theme tokens, selected through
the one feature query this stylesheet allows, and correct with scripts blocked
because nothing about it was ever script.
</objective>

Binding constraints:
- The three radios keep their `name`, their `value`s, their `class="visually-hidden"`
  (never `display:none` — that would drop them from the tab order and break
  keyboard selection), their `form="settings-form"` and their `checked` computation.
- `role="radiogroup"` + `aria-labelledby` + `aria-describedby` on the row stay
  exactly as they are. A `<legend>` outside a `<fieldset>` is invalid markup with
  no group semantics, which is why the `<h2>` carries the name.
- The `<svg>` itself is `aria-hidden="true" focusable="false"` — the accessible
  names come from the three labels, exactly as today. A labelled graphic would
  announce the runways a second time.
- The `<svg>` carries an explicit size route (a sized class or intrinsic
  width/height). `layout.icon_html()`'s own docstring records what happens
  otherwise: 300x150 and a blown layout.
- Every shape takes its paint from a class bound to a theme token. No colour
  literal. A shape with no class and no fill paints SVG-default black and is
  invisible in dark mode.
- The selected strip's treatment joins the ONE `@supports selector(:has(*))` block
  and reuses the accent entries `.runway-card--selected` already holds on the
  header comment's reservation list. No new accent consumer.
- `images_available` and the `/runway-image/{id}.png` route keep working. The
  three PNGs stay on disk and stay served.
- Both the server-rendered `--selected` class and the live `:has(input:checked)`
  state continue to exist — the first is the no-`:has()` fallback and the
  "this is what is saved" marker.
- Every interpolated value goes through `escape_html()`; registry labels are
  translated at the display site via `i18n.t()`.
