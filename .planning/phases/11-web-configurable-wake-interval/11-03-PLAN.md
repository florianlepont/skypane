<objective>
Add the Wake interval field to the companion Settings page: a fifth `.theme-status` group
containing this codebase's first plain `<input type="number">`, wired into the existing
single merged form and absorbed into `handle_post()`'s single `save_device_config()` call
through an explicit string-to-int conversion gate (D-05, 11-UI-SPEC.md's locked
Interaction and Copywriting contracts).

Purpose: this is the whole user-facing point of the phase — the field that replaces an
SSH edit to `/opt/skypane/skypane.env` plus a `skypane-byos.service` restart. It is also
the first int-typed field this form has ever carried, which is why the conversion gate is
called out as its own named acceptance criterion rather than left implicit.

Output: three locked-copy constants, `wake_interval_group()`, the `render()` and
`handle_post()` wiring, the two mechanical test repairs the new group and the widened
config dict break by construction, new page-level coverage, and the design-system
register entries recording the new control shape.
