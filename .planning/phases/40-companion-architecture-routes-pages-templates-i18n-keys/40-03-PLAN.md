| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-40-06 | Tampering (CSRF) | POST dispatch | mitigate | post_origin_ok() stays the first statement of the POST path, before routes.match(); test_post_origin.py re-run unmodified |
| T-40-07 | Information Disclosure | static_files lookup | mitigate | exact dict-key lookup in STATIC_ROUTES; no request-derived path is ever joined to STATIC_DIR |
| T-40-08 | Elevation of Privilege | gated image routes | mitigate | gallery/runway/illustration/theme-preview stay table entries with auth_required=True and their own membership checks, never STATIC_ROUTES |
