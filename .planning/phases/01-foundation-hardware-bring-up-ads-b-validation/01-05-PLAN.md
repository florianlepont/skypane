<objective>
Build the device side of the Walking Skeleton: a complete, flashable ESP-IDF firmware that wakes, joins Wi-Fi, polls the stub server from plan 01-02, verifies and blits a panel image, persists the hash, and deep-sleeps - backing off exponentially on any failure.

The work is mostly subtraction and configuration, not invention. The reference project already solved the hard parts: a native ESP-IDF driver for this exact dual-controller Spectra 6 panel, a streamed 960 KB download with SHA-256 verification, and a board profile that already targets the XIAO ESP32-S3 Plus on the EE02 driver board. What this plan adds is the trim - removing BLE provisioning, OTA, pairing and QR, none of which DEVICE-03 or DEVICE-05 need - and a fixed log-line contract so that plans 01-06 and 01-07 can verify hardware behaviour against a captured log rather than against someone's recollection.

Two hazards are treated as known traps, not hypotheticals. The EE02 profile is flagged by its own authors as never having been driven against real hardware, so plan 01-06's first successful blit is the verification event. And on this board the panel's master chip-select and power-enable lines are the same GPIOs as UART0's receive and transmit, so a console left on UART drives the panel instead of printing - which shows up as a display artefact rather than an obvious error.

Purpose: DEVICE-03 in full. Every clause of it - wake on schedule, poll over the network, download and display when the image changed, return to deep sleep, back off exponentially on failure - is implemented here.
Output: A flashable `inkframe.bin` built against the EE02 board profile, with a machine-checkable log contract.
</objective>

| T-01-05-03 | Tampering | Display-response field validation | medium | mitigate | The response is validated before any field is copied, per the protocol contract: the image hash must be the sha256-prefixed 64-lowercase-hex form, the sleep interval must be an integer within 1 through 4294967295, the reset field must be a boolean, and URLs must be non-empty http or https strings.
