<objective>
Make the CFG-86 measurement possible at all, then take its "before" reading against the
unmodified tree.

Playwright and Chromium are absent from this worktree (`server/.venv/bin/python3 -c "import
playwright"` raises `ModuleNotFoundError`), and `companion/test_browser_ux.py` is deliberately
designed to SKIP with exit 0 rather than fail red when they are. That means every browser-level
proof CFG-85 needs and the CFG-86 instrument itself are currently silently absent from
`scripts/run_all_tests.py`'s verdict. This plan closes that, and captures the one number the rest
of the phase is judged against.

Question 2: the same file's own in-code comment records a FOURTH historical number, 4276px at
390px, illustrating how these drift phase to phase).

Purpose: CFG-86 is a measurement-and-report requirement. A delta computed against a number taken
by a different tree at a different moment is not a delta.
Output: Playwright + Chromium installed in `server/.venv`; `30-BASELINE.md` carrying the genuine
pre-change reading at both phone widths with the SHA it was taken at.
</objective>
