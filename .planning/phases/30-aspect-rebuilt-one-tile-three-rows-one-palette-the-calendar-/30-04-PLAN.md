that comment currently predicts the emptying and would otherwise become a false statement in the
file.

**2. CFG-85's "every row open with scripts blocked" is met by a different, stronger mechanism.**
Plan 30-03's `<no_js_floor_note>` sets this out in full. Summary: a grouped `<details name>` set is
mutually exclusive in the browser by construction, so server-emitting `open` on all four rows does
not produce four open rows — and the developer approved a sketch with exactly one row open. Row 1
carries `open`; rows 2-4 do not. What is preserved, and is strictly stronger than a visible stack, is
that a closed row's radios stay in the DOM and DO submit, and `<summary>` is natively activatable
with scripts blocked. Plan 30-08 proves all of that. Re-state this discrepancy in the SUMMARY,
flagged for the developer; do NOT quietly satisfy the literal wording by adding a script, which would
break 30-UI-SPEC.md Floor #1 and #6.
