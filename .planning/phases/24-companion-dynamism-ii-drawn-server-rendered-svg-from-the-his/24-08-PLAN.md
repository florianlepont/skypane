<objective>
Four drawings exist by now: a ring on two pages, an enriched battery chart, a day
band on Home, and a regularity grid on Health. D4 is the composition that makes
Home's top read as one thing instead of four stacked things.

The requirement is not decorative. "The Home hero the others feed" is a structural
claim with exactly one failure mode: a hero that looks composed but is built from
its own private copies of the ring and the band, which then drift from the
originals the first time either is fixed. This codebase has already paid for that
once — `companion/battery.py` exists because `battery_percent()` had been copied,
and `_status_tiles_html()`'s docstring records a duplicated frame verdict that took
a plan to remove.

So this plan's central check is behavioural, not structural: break the shared
emitter, and the hero must break with it.

Output: one hero composition on Home, assembled from calls, stacking cleanly at
360 px, with the page's two standing invariants (one frame verdict, one read
reused) asserted rather than assumed.
</objective>

<decisions_covered>
CFG-44 in full. This plan may MOVE the day band 24-06 placed and the ring 24-04
placed into the hero; it may not re-implement either. Health is untouched. Whether
the battery chart appears on Home is **not** in scope — it is Health's, it is
full-width by design-system contract, and duplicating it on Home would be a second
copy of the most expensive drawing in the phase.
</decisions_covered>
