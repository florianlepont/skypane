<objective>
Health's battery chart is the most carefully-reasoned piece of rendering in this
app. Its coordinate scheme, its fixed y-range, its label derivation, its density
rule and its keyboard path were each arrived at by fixing a real defect, and every
one of them is recorded in a docstring or a comment. D8 adds three things to it:
an area under the line, an explicitly marked last point, and a drawn low-battery
threshold.

The whole risk of this plan is collateral damage. Three of the six properties above
are easy to break while adding an area — and one of them, the percentage coordinate
scheme, is the thing that makes the chart survive 360 px at all.

There is one genuine unknown, and it is named here rather than assumed away:
**percentages are not permitted in a `<polyline>`/`<polygon>` `points` list**, which
is why the line is `n-1` `<line>` segments rather than one polyline. The same
constraint binds the obvious way to draw a filled area. Task 1 resolves it by
experiment, with a stated fallback, rather than by a plan pretending to know.

Output: the same chart, plotting the same series, with three additions that each
derive from the pair list the chart already builds.
</objective>

Binding constraints:
- The outer canvas keeps NO viewBox and NO preserveAspectRatio. Non-negotiable.
- Everything new derives from the existing `pairs` list. Do not build a second
  list, do not re-filter, do not index into the raw rows.
- The threshold's y comes from `_point_y()`. A threshold drawn from its own
  arithmetic will drift from the readings by the inset.
- The threshold value comes from `companion/battery.py`. Do not type a millivolt
  number in this file.
- The density rule still applies: above the threshold, cosmetic dots are suppressed.
  Decide explicitly whether the MARKED last point is suppressed too — it should not
  be (that is the whole point of marking it), which means it is an exception to the
  density rule and the exception must be written down where the rule is.
- The keyboard path is untouched: every point keeps its hit target, its tabindex,
  its `<title>` and its `aria-label`.
- The `""` return below two points stays. An area under one point is meaningless.
- Labels stay OUTSIDE the SVG as `<span>`s. The threshold's label is a `<span>` in
  the existing grid, not an SVG `<text>` — adding SVG text here would reintroduce
  the viewBox-overflow class of defect the wrapper grid exists to prevent.
- `style.css` stays at zero stray comment terminators; new comments go inside a
  block comment.
