## Reconciliation Note (2026-08-26, post-execution)

**This plan was written against a design that changed underneath it before this plan ever ran.** It assumed the Wave 2/Wave 3 design current at the time it was drafted: Zilla Slab typography, a two-tone Floyd-Steinberg-dithered "mood" background per state with flat quiet-zone caption plates, and a single flat-fill silhouette mirrored by state (D-10). None of that is what shipped. Between this plan's drafting and its execution, D-20 through D-27 (`03-CONTEXT.md` addenda 2-3) replaced typography with **PT Serif Regular** (not Bold — D-27, reopening exactly the hairline-legibility risk the original Zilla Slab pick was engineered to avoid), replaced the dithered mood background with a **flat single-color field** (D-21 — no gradient, no quiet zones, because there is nothing left to protect), and **dropped illustration mirroring entirely** (D-24 — every illustration renders nose-left in both states). What follows re-scopes this plan's on-glass battery to verify the design that actually shipped.

**Task 1's scope is corrected below** (its colour-contract addendum now points at `03-UI-SPEC.md` Revision 4 rather than the retired dithered-background/quiet-zone design, and its calibration-preview acceptance criterion is corrected to match the shipped single-file return). **Task 2's verification battery is corrected below** (the font-legibility step is retargeted at PT Serif Regular with the specific hairline risk named as the headline item and `PTSerif-Bold.ttf` named as the documented fallback; a new bezel-clipping step is added; the tracking and quiet-zone-plate questions are dropped because neither mechanism exists; the state-distinction step is rewritten around the fact that nose direction is no longer a state cue). **Task 3's tuning targets are corrected below** (`MOOD_LIGHT_TINT_MIX`, `MOOD_NOISE_AMPLITUDE`, and `QUIET_ZONE_PAD` were all **removed** from the shipped code along with the mood-background/quiet-zone mechanisms they tuned; only the two still-uncalibrated palette triples — Yellow and Red — remain open for this plan's calibration pass, since Blue and Green were separately developer-confirmed by D-21 and re-tuning them here would revert a locked decision).

<expectation_note id="N-06-01-01">
**A forced render stays on the glass until the next real detection.** `poll_loop.py` holds the last panel through detection gaps by design (confirmed in 02-05 Step 7). Every forcing step below therefore ends the same way: restart `inkframe-poll.timer`. If the session ends with that timer stopped, the frame sits on a synthetic test image indefinitely and looks broken. That is why the timer's final status is an acceptance criterion rather than an instruction buried in a step.

**Each on-glass observation costs a full panel refresh.** The measured hardware full-refresh time is ~31.5 seconds, and the device wakes on its own poll cycle. Batch the checks in the order given below rather than iterating one tweak at a time.
</expectation_note>
